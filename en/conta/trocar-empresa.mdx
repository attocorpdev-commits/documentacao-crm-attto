One account can belong to several companies: the ones you created and the ones whose team invited you. The CRM always works with one company at a time, the **active company**. To see another company's data, switch it in the selector.

<Note>
  Each company has its own channels, contacts, and team. Nothing is shared with your other companies.
</Note>

## Who sees the selector

Everyone sees the company selector. The list shows:

- The companies you created, marked with a crown. Hover over it to see **You are the owner**.
- The companies where you are an active team member.

If you belong to a single company, the list shows only that one.

## Switch on a computer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the selector">
    At the top of the side menu, click the card with the active company name and the text **Select company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    In the list, the active company shows the **active** tag. Click the company you want to open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The selector disappears when the menu is collapsed. Click **Expand** to open the menu and see the selector again.
</Tip>

## Switch on a phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    In the bottom bar, tap **More**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the company">
    In the **More options** panel, tap the company field at the top and choose the company from the list. The green dot next to the name means the company has a connected channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes when you switch

- Every screen starts showing the new company's data: conversations, Pipeline, contacts, Dashboard, channels, and AI.
- If you were inside a conversation, contact, or agent, the CRM goes back to that section's list, already in the new company.
- The menu shows the areas you are allowed to use in that company. Your role and permissions can be different in each company.
- The CRM remembers the last company you opened in this browser. Next time you sign in, it opens as the active company.

<Warning>
  While the CRM is saving something in the active company, switching is blocked and you see **Please wait: we are saving in this company. You can switch companies right after.** If the AI questionnaire is open, you see **Close the AI questionnaire to switch companies.** This way nothing is saved in the wrong company.
</Warning>

## Open a conversation from another company

When you open the link to a WhatsApp or Instagram conversation that belongs to another of your companies, the CRM switches companies on its own. During the switch you see **Opening the conversation in its company...**.

If it does not work, you see **We could not open the conversation in that company right now.** Click **Back to conversations** and try again.

## Create or rename a company

If you own a company, the selector also lets you:

- **New company**: opens the **New company** window. Type the name and click **Create**. The new company becomes the active company right away.
- **Rename**: shows next to the active company, only when you are its owner. Type the new name and click **Save**.

Before you create it, the window shows the new company's conditions, such as a free trial or the need for its own plan. If you have reached the company limit, you see **Company limit reached**, with the **Contact support** button. See the details in [Plans](/en/conta/planos).

<Note>
  People who only belong to companies as team members, without owning any, do not see the **New company** option.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I was invited to a company, but it does not show in the selector.">
    The company shows once your team access is active. Ask the company owner to check your details in [Team](/en/conta/equipe).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do some screens disappear when I switch companies?">
    Permissions apply per company. The owner of each company decides what you see there. Some screens also depend on the active company's plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I get alerts from every company?">
    You choose. In [Profile](/en/conta/perfil), in the **Companies** block of the notifications, check which companies you want to receive alerts from.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The selector is greyed out and will not let me switch.">
    The CRM is saving something in the active company or the AI questionnaire is open. Wait for it to finish or close the questionnaire and try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
