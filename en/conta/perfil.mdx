In your profile you manage what is yours inside the CRM: name, status, system appearance and the alerts you want to receive.

## How to access

Click your photo or initial, in the top right corner of the screen, and choose **My Profile**.

The **My Profile** screen has these options:

| Option | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | See and edit your details. |
| **Preferences** | Choose status, theme and language. |
| **Subscription** | Track your plan and AI conversations. This is also where you change plans. See [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos). |
| **Contact support** | Open a conversation with support on WhatsApp. |
| **Sign out** | End your session. |

Inside each option, click **Back** to return.

<Card title="Open My Profile in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/profile/perfil" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Edit your name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Profile section">
    In **My Profile**, click **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the pencil">
    Next to your name, click **Edit name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new name">
    Fill in **Display name**. The limit is 24 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This name appears in your profile, in your avatar and in conversations.

In the same section, under **Account Information**, you see your **Email** and your **Account ID**. Use the buttons next to them to copy.

## Change your password

Still in the **Profile** section, the **Security** block (**Password and two-step verification**) lets you change your password without signing out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the password change">
    In the **Security** block, click the **Change password** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it is you">
    In the **Change password** window, fill in **Current password**. Before saving, wait for the window's security check box to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the new password">
    Fill in **New password** and **Confirm new password**. The new password must have at least 10 characters, with one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character, and be different from the current one. The **Password requirements** list shows what is still missing. Very common passwords, or passwords that contain your name or e-mail, are rejected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save new password**. You see the **Password changed** notice, and the new password takes effect on your next sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Click **Show passwords** to check what you typed.
</Tip>

<Note>
  If you see **Current password is incorrect.**, check the password you use to sign in today. If you do not remember it, sign out and use **I forgot my password** on the sign-in screen.
</Note>

### Two-step verification

The same **Security** block holds **Two-step verification**, in a box separate from the password, which asks for a code from the authenticator app in addition to the password. See how to turn it on in [Two-step verification](/en/conta/seguranca).

### Badges

Still in the **Profile** section, the **Badges** block shows your **Current level**. Your level rises with subscription time and AI usage. Members of the ambassador program also see the **Ambassador** badge. See [Ambassador program](/en/empresa/embaixadores).

## Presence status

Your status shows the team whether you are available.

| Status | Color |
| --- | --- |
| **Online** | Green |
| **Away** | Yellow |
| **Offline** | Gray |

There are two ways to change it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="From the profile menu">
    Click your photo or initial and choose an option under **Status**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From preferences">
    In **My Profile**, open **Preferences** and choose under **Status**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The colored dot on your photo shows your current status.

<Note>
  For team members, after 25 minutes without using the system the question **Are you still there?** appears. Click **Continue** to stay available, or **Set me as away**. If there is no answer within 2 minutes, the status changes to **Away** and you stop receiving new conversations from the queue. When you come back, the **Welcome back** window offers **Resume work** and **Stay as away**.
</Note>

## Theme and language

In **Preferences** you also choose:

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**.
- **Language**: **Português**, **Español** or **English**.

## Notifications

Click the bell, in the top right corner. The red number shows how many notifications you have not read yet.

The **Notifications** window has three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Recent** | The latest alerts. Click an alert to go straight to it. |
| **Channels** | The status of each channel. If one is disconnected, click **Reconnect**. |
| **Preferences** | What you want to receive. |

To clear pending alerts, click **Mark all**.

### Get alerts with the CRM closed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open preferences">
    In the bell, click the **Preferences** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on for this device">
    In **Push on this device**, click **Enable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow it in the browser">
    Accept the permission request your browser shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you see **Permission blocked**, allow notifications in the site settings of your browser. On iPhone and iPad, you need to install the app on the home screen before turning it on.
</Warning>

### Choose the alert types

In **Notification types**, turn each type on or off:

- **Calendar**
- **Activity**
- **New lead**
- **New conversation**
- **Connection**
- **Disconnection**
- **AI conversations**
- **Pipeline**
- **AI transfer**
- **Subscription**
- **WhatsApp message**
- **Undelivered message**
- **Instagram message**

If you belong to more than one company, you also see the **Companies** block. There you choose which companies you want to receive alerts from.

## Settings screen

The profile menu also has the **Settings** option. It is a shortcut screen organized in groups: **Account** (**My Profile**), **Artificial intelligence** (**AI Credits**, **Service protocol** and **AI Agent**), **Team** (**Company Team**), **Channels & Tracking** (**Connections** and **Meta Tracking**) and **Reports**. You only see the areas available to you.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my sign-in email from the profile?">
    No. The **Profile** section shows the email, but only lets you edit the name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change my password?">
    In **My Profile**, open **Profile** and, in the **Security** block, click **Change password**. If you do not remember your current password, sign out and click **I forgot my password** on the sign-in screen. See the step-by-step in [First access](/en/primeiros-passos/primeiro-acesso).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Set your password screen appeared. What do I do?">
    It appears when your account is flagged to change its password, as on first access. Fill in **New password** and **Confirm password**, with at least 10 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character, and click **Save password**. The CRM only opens after that. If the change is flagged while you are already signed in, the screen appears the next time you return to the CRM tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I know if I am on the latest version of the system?">
    Click your photo or initial and choose **Check for update**. If there is something new, the option changes to **Update available**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Offline status stop me from receiving conversations?">
    In the service queue, people who are offline stay in the queue, but may not be available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
