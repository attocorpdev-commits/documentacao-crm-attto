Here you see your plan, when it expires and how many AI conversations you have left. You can also change plans, renew, cancel or reactivate your subscription.

## Available plans

The Atto Corp CRM has three plans: **Start**, **Prime** and **Ultra**.

To see prices and what each plan includes, go to [attocorp.com/#planos](https://attocorp.com/#planos).

## How to access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your photo or initial in the top right corner and choose **My Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Subscription">
    Click **Subscription**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Open Subscription in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/profile/assinatura" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your CRM account.
</Card>

## Plan status

The **Plan Status** block shows the company name, the date your plan expires and the days left.

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Your plan is up to date. |
| **Expiring** | 3 days or less until it expires. |
| **Expired** | The plan has expired and access is blocked. |

If you scheduled a plan downgrade, its notice also shows in this block.

## Renew or upgrade your plan

The **Renew or upgrade plan** block has two buttons:

- **Renew plan**: takes you to the plans page to choose.
- **Change plan**: opens the plan change inside the CRM, without leaving the screen.

Use this path also to add companies, WhatsApp channels and members to your plan.

## Change plan

The **Change plan** button opens the **Change plan** window. There you see the **Start**, **Prime** and **Ultra** plans, an item-by-item comparison and the choice between **Monthly plan** and **Annual plan**.

The window sums up the rule in one sentence: **Upgrade right away, or schedule a downgrade for the end of the current cycle.**

### Move to a bigger plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan change">
    In **My Profile > Subscription**, click **Change plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose monthly or annual">
    At the top of the window, select **Monthly plan** or **Annual plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the plan">
    Click the button for the plan you want and complete the payment on the screen that opens. If you already have a subscription, you see the **Switch to this plan** button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bigger plan takes effect right away, as soon as the payment is confirmed.

<Note>
  The **7-day free trial** button only shows if you never had a plan, and only on the **Monthly plan** option. If you already subscribe, the button is **Switch to this plan**. If you had a plan before and it expired or was canceled, the button is **Subscribe to this plan**. In both cases you are charged normally, with no new trial. While the CRM checks your account status, the button shows **Please wait...**. The same rule applies on the plans page and on the **Choose your plan** screen.
</Note>

On **Ultra**, the button is **Talk to the team**: this plan is tailored to you, so the scope is defined together with Atto.

### Downgrade your plan

Need a smaller plan? The **Reduce plan** block, inside the **Change plan** window, schedules the change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan change">
    In **My Profile > Subscription**, click **Change plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the check">
    The **Reduce plan** block shows **Checking payment method...** for a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the smaller plan">
    Click the button for the plan you want. It shows the plan name, for example **Downgrade to Prime**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Downgrade to the Prime plan?** window repeats what will happen. Click **Confirm downgrade**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the confirmation">
    The CRM shows the **Downgrade scheduled** notice with the date the change takes effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The downgrade takes effect at the end of the current cycle, not right away. You do not get back the amount for the period already paid.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Your extra channels stay connected after the downgrade.
</Note>

Until the end of the cycle you keep your current plan. The **Plan Status** block shows the **Downgrade scheduled to the plan** notice with the plan name and the date.

<Note>
  The **Reduce plan** block only shows when there is a plan smaller than yours. If you are already on **Start**, you do not see this block.
</Note>

### When downgrading is not available

If your subscription is not charged to a card, the block shows the notice **Downgrade is available only for card subscriptions. Contact support to adjust your plan.** and the **Contact support** button, which opens a WhatsApp conversation.

### Cancel a scheduled downgrade

Changed your mind? In the **Plan Status** block, click **Cancel downgrade**. The CRM confirms with **Downgrade canceled** and your current plan continues as normal.

You can cancel at any time until the date the downgrade takes effect.

<Tip>
  If you had a scheduled downgrade and decide to upgrade through the **Change plan** window, the downgrade is canceled automatically.
</Tip>

## Manage subscription

The **Manage subscription** block shows your billing details:

- **Plan**
- **Amount**
- **Billing cycle**: **Monthly**, **Annual** or **7-day trial**
- **Next charge**
- **Payment method**

### Cancel your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the cancellation">
    Click **Cancel subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the notice">
    The **Cancel subscription?** window explains what happens in your case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Cancel subscription** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Your cycle | What happens when you cancel |
| --- | --- |
| **7-day trial** | The trial ends right away and you are not charged. |
| **Monthly**, with automatic renewal | You keep access until the expiration date and are not charged for the next cycle. |

<Note>
  The cancel button only shows on subscriptions with automatic renewal. On an **Annual** plan or a one-time payment, the plan is valid until the expiration date and does not renew. That is why the button does not show.
</Note>

### Reactivate your subscription

Canceled and changed your mind? While your access is still valid, click **Reactivate subscription**. Automatic renewal turns back on.

In some cases the CRM takes you to the plans page to complete the reactivation.

## AI conversations

AI usage is measured in conversations. One conversation equals one contact served within a 24-hour window.

The **AI conversations** block shows how many conversations are left and the percentage available. The balance belongs to the company, not to each user. To learn what uses the balance, where to see the statement and what happens when it runs out, see [Conversations and AI credits](/en/ia/creditos).

<Warning>
  If the month's conversations run out and you do not have an extra pack, the AI pauses until the renewal.
</Warning>

### Top up conversations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the top-up">
    Click **Top up conversations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the pack">
    In **Conversation pack**, select the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and buy">
    Check the **Total** and click **Buy**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Turn on **AI conversations** notifications to get alerts about your balance. See how in [Profile](/en/conta/perfil).
</Tip>

## AI actions history

If you are the account owner, the **Subscription** screen also has the **AI actions history** block: who turned the AI on or off, who cleared its memory, and when.

Each row shows the action (**Turned the AI on**, **Turned the AI off** or **Cleared memory**), the scope (one conversation, the selected conversations, all conversations or all of the agent's conversations), the name of who did it and the date. If no one has made changes yet, you see **No actions recorded yet.**

<Note>
  This block only shows for the owner. See in [Team](/en/conta/equipe) who can turn the AI on and off.
</Note>

## When the account has no plan

If the account has no active plan, the CRM opens the **Choose your plan** screen instead of the system. Choose a plan to unlock access.

If there is a pending charge, the screen is called **Settle your subscription**. Subscribe to a plan to reactivate access. Your data stays saved.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I paid and the screen shows Confirming your payment. Is that normal?">
    Yes. Activation usually takes a few seconds and the screen updates on its own. If it does not activate within a few minutes, contact support with the email used for the purchase. You do not need to pay again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My plan expired. Did I lose my data?">
    No. Access is blocked, but your data stays saved. Renew your plan to use it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I paid the first monthly fee after the trial. Do I need to tell support?">
    No. The CRM recognizes this payment and unlocks the plan on its own, and the account is not blocked because of the trial when the payment has already been made. If access does not come back within a few minutes, contact support with the email used for the purchase. Do not pay again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I canceled the free trial. Can I start another trial?">
    No. The free trial is valid once. To come back, choose a plan. That is why the trial button no longer shows for your account: it becomes **Subscribe to this plan**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I downgraded my plan. Does the change take effect right away?">
    No. The downgrade is scheduled for the end of the current cycle. Until then you use your current plan, and the amount for the period already paid is not refunded. Upgrading, on the other hand, takes effect right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I lose my extra channels when I downgrade?">
    No. The channels you already connected stay connected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Start plan include an Instagram channel?">
    No. The Instagram channel is an extra item on the **Prime** and **Ultra** plans. To use it on **Start**, change your plan first. See [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see the prices?">
    At [attocorp.com/#planos](https://attocorp.com/#planos).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
