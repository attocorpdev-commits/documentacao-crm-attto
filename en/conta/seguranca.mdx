With two-step verification on, the CRM asks for your password and then for a 6-digit code from the authenticator app on your phone. Even if someone finds out your password, they cannot get in without this code.

<Tip>
  Recommended for business owners: it protects the account even if the password leaks.
</Tip>

## Before you start

Install an authenticator app on your phone. For example, **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator** and **1Password** all work.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Profile">
    Click your photo or initial, in the top right corner, and choose **My Profile**. In the **Profile** section, scroll down to the **Password** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start turning it on">
    In **Two-step verification**, the badge shows **Off**. Click **Turn on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    In the **Turn on two-step verification** window, open the authenticator app and scan the QR code.

    Can't scan it? Copy the key shown below the QR code with **Copy key** and type it in the app. On your phone, you can also tap **Open in authenticator app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the code">
    Type the 6 digits the app shows and click **Confirm code**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CRM confirms with **Two-step verification turned on** and the badge changes to **On**. From your next sign-in on, the code will be requested.

<Card title="Open My Profile in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/profile">
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Sign in with the code

After the password, or after signing in with Google, the **Two-step verification** screen appears.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the authenticator app">
    Look for the Atto Corp code. It changes every few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type and verify">
    Type the 6 digits and click **Verify**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CRM only opens after the right code. To switch accounts, click **Sign out and use another account**.

## Turn it off

In **My Profile**, on the **Password** card, click **Turn off**. The **Turn off two-step verification?** window warns that the account will go back to asking only for the password. Confirm with **Turn off**.

<Warning>
  We recommend keeping verification on. Without it, anyone who has your password can get into your account.
</Warning>

## Team security

On the [Team](/en/conta/equipe) screen, the **Team security** card shows who on the team already has verification on (**Verification on** or **No verification**) and each person's last access.

<Note>
  The **Require two-step verification from the team** option cannot be turned on yet. For now, the card lets you check who has already turned it on.
</Note>

When your company starts requiring verification, anyone who has not turned it on yet will see the **Your company requires two-step verification** screen when signing in, and will only access the company after turning it on.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Type the 6 digits from the authenticator app.** | The field is empty or incomplete. Type the 6 digits. |
| **Invalid or expired code. Check the app and try again.** | The code changed or was typed wrong. Use the code that shows in the app right now. Also check that your phone's time is set to automatic. |
| **We could not verify your security** | Connection failure. Check your internet and click **Try again**. For security, access is only allowed after this check. |
| **Could not complete it. Try again in a moment.** | Temporary error. Wait a few seconds and try again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I changed or lost my phone. How do I sign in?">
    Without the authenticator app, the CRM does not allow access. Contact [Support](/en/empresa/suporte) and include your account email.

    Before changing phones, transfer the accounts in your authenticator app to the new device, or turn off verification and turn it on again on the new phone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The code does not work, even when typed correctly.">
    The code depends on your phone's time. Set the device's date and time to automatic and try with a new code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to type the code every time?">
    The code is requested on every new sign-in to the account, including when signing in with Google.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
