If you do not remember your password, create a new one from the login screen itself. The CRM sends a 6-digit code to your email and, with it, you choose the new password.

<Note>
  Remember your current password and just want to change it? Do it with your account open, in [Profile](/en/conta/perfil).
</Note>

## Create a new password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the recovery">
    On the login screen, right below the **Password** field, click **I forgot my password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    On the **Forgot your password?** screen, fill in **Email** with your account email. Wait for the security check box, right above the button, to finish and click **Send code**.

    The **Request received!** notice appears. For security, it says the code was sent if there is an account with that email, without confirming whether the account exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">
    Open the email with the subject **Código de recuperação de senha - Atto Corp - CRM**. On the **Enter the code** screen, type the 6 digits and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the new password">
    On the **New password** screen, fill in **New password** and **Confirm new password**. Click **Reset password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with the new password">
    The **Password reset!** notice appears and you go back to the login screen. Sign in with your email and the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The code is valid for 15 minutes. When you request a new code, the previous one stops working. Always use the latest one you received.
</Warning>

## New password rules

As you type, the **Password requirements** list checks each rule you meet:

- **At least 10 characters**
- **One uppercase letter**
- **One lowercase letter**
- **One number**
- **One special character (e.g. ! @ # -)**

The password also cannot be too common, like words such as "password" or keyboard sequences, and cannot contain your name or your email.

<Tip>
  Click the eye icon inside the **New password** field to check what you typed.
</Tip>

## Didn't get the code?

- Check your spam and promotions folders.
- Check that the email you typed is the one on your account. For security, the screen looks the same even when the email has no account, and in that case no code arrives.
- On the **Enter the code** screen, wait for the **Resend code in** countdown to finish and for the security check box to complete. Then click **Resend code**. The **Request sent again!** notice appears.

To fix the email, click **← Back**. To give up, click **Back to login** on the first screen.

## Two-step verification

Resetting your password does not turn off two-step verification. If it is on for your account, after you sign in with the new password the CRM still asks for the 6-digit code from the authenticator app, on the **Two-step verification** screen. Learn more in [Security](/en/conta/seguranca).

<Note>
  Password recovery only changes the password. If you lost the phone with the authenticator app, contact [Support](/en/empresa/suporte).
</Note>

## Old recovery link

If you opened a password recovery link you received by email, the CRM shows the **Reset password** screen. Fill in **New password** and **Confirm new password** and click **Reset password**. When it is done, **Password changed successfully!** appears and the CRM goes back to the login.

If the link is no longer valid, you see **Invalid or expired link. Request a new recovery link on the login screen.** Click **Go to login** and follow the steps in [Create a new password](#create-a-new-password).

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Incomplete code** | Type all 6 digits of the code. |
| **Wrong code** | The code is wrong or more than 15 minutes have passed. Check the latest email or request a new one with **Resend code**. |
| **Passwords do not match** | Type the same password in **New password** and **Confirm new password**. |
| **Your password cannot contain your name or e-mail.** | Choose a password without parts of your name or your email. |
| **Password is too common. Avoid words like "password" or keyboard sequences.** | Create a less predictable password. |
| **This password has appeared in known data leaks. Choose another one.** | Shown on the **Reset password** screen. Choose a password you have never used. |
| **The new password must be different from the current one.** | Shown on the **Reset password** screen. Choose a password different from the one you used. |
| Too many attempts | Too many requests were made in a row. Wait for the time shown and try again. |
| **Could not send the code. Please try again.** | Temporary error. Wait a few seconds and try again. |
| **We could not complete the security check. Please try again.** | The security check box did not finish. Click **Try again**, right below the message. |
| **We could not confirm the security check. Refresh the page and try again.** | Reload the page and request the code again. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long is the code valid?">
    15 minutes. After that, request a new one with **Resend code**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I requested the code twice. Which one do I use?">
    Use the latest one you received. Each new request cancels the previous code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I tried to create an account and saw Email already registered. What do I do?">
    Your email already has a CRM account. Go back to the login and, if you do not remember the password, click **I forgot my password**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I typed the wrong password several times at login. What happens?">
    After 5 wrong attempts in a row, login is blocked for 30 seconds. If you do not remember the password, use **I forgot my password** instead of trying again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am on a company's team. Can I reset my password myself?">
    Yes. Recovery works for anyone with a CRM account, using the email you sign in with.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
