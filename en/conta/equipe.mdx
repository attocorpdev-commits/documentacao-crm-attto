On the team screen you register the people who work with you in the CRM. You also set which screens and which channels each person can use.

## How to access

In the side menu, open **Settings** and click **Company Team**.

The screen has two tabs: **Members** and **Teams**.

<Note>
  Only the owner can manage members. Other users can see the list but cannot change it.
</Note>

<Card title="Open Company Team in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/team" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Add a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On the **Members** tab, click **Add member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Fill in **Full name** and **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the permissions">
    In **Permission packages**, click **Agent** or **Manager**. Then, if you want, adjust item by item in **Allowed pages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set channel access">
    In **WhatsApp channel access**, choose **All**, **Mine** or **None** for each channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click **Create and add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CRM sends a welcome email to the new member. On their first sign-in they see the **Set your password** screen, and they only get in after creating their own password. After that they can change the password whenever they want in **My Profile**. See [Profile](/en/conta/perfil).

<Warning>
  If the email cannot be sent, the window shows the email and the temporary password. Copy them and pass them to the member through a secure channel.
</Warning>

## Roles

Each person appears in the list with a role badge.

| Role | Who it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The owner of the company account. |
| **Manager** | Member with a management role. |
| **Support Agent** | Role of every new member. |

What each member sees in the system is set by permissions, explained below.

## Page permissions

Packages are shortcuts. They fill in the permissions at once, and you can still change each item.

| Package | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | Basic customer service. No Team, Settings, Connections, Broadcasts or AI. |
| **Manager** | Broad access: everything in Agent + Broadcasts, Connections, Settings, Team and AI. |

In **Allowed pages**, check only the screens the person should see. Unchecked screens do not appear in their menu.

<Tip>
  For the member to be able to connect their own number, check **Create own WhatsApp channel**.
</Tip>

## Channel access

For each channel, choose what the member sees:

| Option | What the member sees |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | All conversations in the channel. |
| **Mine** | Only the conversations assigned to them. |
| **None** | Nothing. They have no access to this channel. |

A member with no channel appears in the list with the notice **No channel assigned**.

## Who can control the AI

Turning the AI on and off and erasing its memory are no longer owner-only actions. Any member with the AI pages enabled can also:

- In [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia), in the **Turn the AI on or off per conversation** block, click **Pick conversations** and turn the AI on or off in the chosen conversations or in all of them.
- Inside a conversation, click **Reset AI memory** so the AI forgets the history of that conversation.

What changes from person to person is the reach: someone with only a few channels assigned acts only on the conversations in those channels.

<Note>
  When the member cannot see the conversations, the window says: **You can't see the AI state of these conversations. Turning it on or off is still available: the change applies to the conversations your permission covers.** The buttons stay active, within their permission.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The owner can track who changed the AI in the **AI actions history** block, in **My Profile > Subscription**. See [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).
</Tip>

## Edit a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the permissions">
    In the list, click the pencil icon next to the member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the changes">
    Change the **Allowed pages**, the **WhatsApp channel access** and the **Instagram channel access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the queue">
    In **Support queue**, check **Participates in the support queue** if the member should receive conversations in the automatic distribution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save permissions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the removal">
    Click the trash icon next to the member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who takes over the contacts">
    In **Reassign contacts to**, select another member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Reassign and remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The member's access is revoked immediately. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

If there is no other member to receive the contacts, add a member before removing.

## Teams

Teams let you group people, such as Sales and Support, and link each group to the channels it handles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the team">
    On the **Teams** tab, click **New team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter the **Team name** and, if you want, the **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the channels">
    Check the channels in **Linked channels** and in **Linked Instagram channels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the people">
    On the team card, click **Members**, check who belongs to it and click **Save members**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a team, use **Edit**. To remove it, use **Delete**.

A team with no channel shows the notice **No channel linked**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I got a message saying the member limit was reached.">
    Each plan allows a certain number of members. The top of the **Members** tab shows how many you already use. To increase it, click **Contact support**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The system says the person is already a member.">
    That email is already part of your team. Find the person in the list and edit their permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The person already had a CRM account. Do they get a new password?">
    No. They are added to the team and keep using the password they already had.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit or remove the owner?">
    No. The edit and remove icons do not appear for the owner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
