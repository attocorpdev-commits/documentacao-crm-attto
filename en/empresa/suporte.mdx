## Ask for help from the CRM

The fastest way is to open support from inside the CRM. That way the message already arrives with your name, role and email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Support">
    Click the CRM's support button, or any **Talk to support** button. The **Support** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    In the **Describe the problem** field, tell us what happened. Example: "I can't add a contact".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Call support**. WhatsApp opens with the message ready. Just send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the same window, the **Documentation** card opens this help center and the **Updates** card shows what is new in the system.

<Tip>
  To speed things up, describe the problem and tell us the email you use in the CRM. If you can, send a screenshot and the time it happened.
</Tip>

## What support covers

| Topic | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Availability** | The CRM has a target of 99.5% availability per month. |
| **Scheduled maintenance** | Announced 48 hours in advance. |
| **Critical incidents** | Handled within 4 business hours. |
| **Not covered by support** | Consulting, content creation, campaigns and training. |
