## Privacy Policy

The policy follows the LGPD (Law No. 13,709/2018) and the Marco Civil da Internet (Law No. 12,965/2014). It opens with a summary in plain language.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What the policy covers">
    Who we are, who the policy applies to and who decides what, data we process, purposes and legal bases, artificial intelligence, sharing and sub-processors, international transfer, information security, security incidents, sensitive data and data of children and teenagers, cookies and local storage, retention and deletion, your rights as a data subject, changes, Google integration and Limited Use, and contact.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cookies">
    On your first visit, the **Cookies & Privacy** notice appears. Cookies are split into four categories:

    - **Necessary**: login, security and language. Always on.
    - **Functional**: remember preferences like theme and layout.
    - **Analytics**: help us understand errors and usage to improve the product.
    - **Marketing**: affiliate referral attribution.

    Everything that is not necessary starts off and only turns on if you accept. On the public pages of the website, with your consent, Atto uses ad measurement and browsing analytics tools. Inside the system, after you sign in, they do not load. See the [Cookie Policy](https://app.attocorp.com/cookies).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long data is kept">
    After the account is closed, you have 30 days to export your data. After that, it is deleted. Access logs are kept for 6 months and tax data for 5 years.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Cookie Policy

The [Cookie Policy](https://app.attocorp.com/cookies) lists each cookie the CRM and the website use, with category, duration and purpose. It complements the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Cookie Policy">
    Go to [app.attocorp.com/cookies](https://app.attocorp.com/cookies). The link also shows in the **Cookies & Privacy** notice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your choice">
    Click **Manage preferences**. Turn each category on or off and click **Save choices**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your choice lasts 1 year. After that, the CRM asks again.

<Card title="seguranca@attocorp.com" icon="shield-halved" horizontal href="mailto:seguranca@attocorp.com">
