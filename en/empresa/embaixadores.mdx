The Atto Ambassador Program is for people who refer the CRM. You get a link and a coupon that are yours alone, and you track in your own dashboard who clicked, who signed up and who became a customer.

<Info>
  The ambassador dashboard is separate from the CRM and has its own sign-in, at [app.attocorp.com/embaixadores](https://app.attocorp.com/embaixadores).
</Info>

## How to join the program

Joining the program is handled by the Atto Corp team. There is no open sign-up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Atto creates your access">
    The team registers your email, your link and your coupon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Open the welcome email. On the **Set password** screen, choose a password with at least 6 characters and click **Set password and sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to the dashboard">
    From then on, go to [app.attocorp.com/embaixadores](https://app.attocorp.com/embaixadores), enter your email and password and click **Sign in**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the email link has expired, the screen says: "The link may have expired. Ask the administrator for a new one." Ask Atto Corp for a new link.

  An account that is not an ambassador account cannot get into the dashboard. The screen shows **Access denied**.
</Note>

## Your link and your coupon

In the dashboard, open **Settings**. There you find **Your ambassador link** and **Your discount coupon**, each with a **Copy** button.

| Way to refer | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Link** | The link has the format `app.attocorp.com/i/your-name`. Anyone who opens the link goes straight to the plans, and the referral is stored in that person's browser for up to 1 year. |
| **Coupon** | At sign-up, the person types the coupon in the **Coupon (optional)** field. The CRM confirms with "Valid coupon, applied to your referral." |

<Tip>
  In **Sales materials** you find ready-made texts to copy: **WhatsApp / direct message**, **Post / Stories (Instagram)** and **Short referral**.
</Tip>

## The dashboard

The **Dashboard** shows how your link and your coupon are performing. Use the **This Month**, **Last 30 days** or **All** filter.

| Indicator | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Clicks** | How many times your link was opened. |
| **Sign-ups** | How many people signed up through your referral. |
| **Active customers** | Referrals with an active plan. |
| **To receive** | Commission amount not yet paid. |
| **Received** | Amount already paid to you. |
| **Next payment** | Date of the next payout. |

<Note>
  Until payouts are active on your account, the amount cards show **Coming soon**.
</Note>

### Conversions

The **Conversions** table lists each referral with **Date**, **Customer**, **Plan** and **Status** (**Active**, **Inactive** or **No plan**).

For data protection (LGPD), the dashboard shows only the customer's first name and never displays CPF, email or phone. At sign-up, the referred person chooses whether to let the person who referred them know. If they do not allow it, they appear as **Anonymous**.

### Payouts

The **Payout history** table shows each payout by **Period**, **Amount** and **Status**:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting your invoice** | The payout has been closed and is waiting for your invoice. |
| **To be paid** | The invoice was received and the payment is scheduled. |
| **Paid** | The payout was paid. The payment date appears next to it. |

There is no withdrawal button. Atto Corp closes the cycle, records your invoice and makes the payment.

## Your details

In **Settings**, the **Your details** block shows **Name**, **Coupon** and **Status** (**Active** or **Paused**).

## Benefits in the CRM

If you also use the CRM, the **Badges** block in **My Profile** shows the **Ambassador** badge. When your Start plan comes from the program, it shows "Start plan included via the Ambassadors program". Your **Current level** rises with subscription time and AI usage. With 5 years or more of active subscription, you earn the **Infinity Member** badge.

## Questions
