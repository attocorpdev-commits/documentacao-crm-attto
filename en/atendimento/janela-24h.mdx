The 24-hour window is a Meta rule for Official WhatsApp (Meta). It defines when you can send a free-form message to the contact and when you can only send an approved template.

<Info>
  This page applies only to **Official WhatsApp (Meta)**. WhatsApp (QR Code) has no window: you can reply at any time. See the difference in [WhatsApp types](/en/primeiros-passos/tipos-whatsapp).
</Info>

## How the window works

- The window opens when the contact sends a message to your number.
- It stays open for 24 hours, counted from the **contact's last message**.
- While it is open, you reply with text, audio, images and files, like in any conversation.
- After 24 hours without a new message from the contact, the window closes. From then on, only a Meta-approved **template** can be sent.
- When the contact writes again, the window opens again for another 24 hours.

<Note>
  Your own messages do not reopen the window. Only the contact's message counts.
</Note>

### Contact who never wrote

If the contact has never sent a message on that channel, the window starts closed. To start the conversation, you need to send a template.

In that case, the conversation area shows **This contact has no conversation on this channel yet. Send a template to start.**

## How the CRM shows a closed window

When the window is closed, the conversation changes like this:

| What you see | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| The **Outside the 24h window** notice, saying only templates are available, with the **Send Template** button | Above the message field |
| A text saying the 24h window has expired and you should use a template | Inside the message field |
| Message field, **\+** button (**Attachments and actions**), emoji button, record audio button and send button locked | In the send bar |
| Reactions turned off, with **Reaction unavailable: more than 24 hours have passed since the customer's last message.** | In the message's **More options** menu |

On some official channels the notice has a different text: it says the customer must reply to reopen the conversation, and that to start one you send a template approved through the channel's template flow. That notice does not have the **Send Template** button. In that case, wait for the contact to reply.

<Note>
  On WhatsApp the CRM does not show a countdown of the time left. The notice appears once the window has closed. To know whether it is still open, check the time of the contact's last message.
</Note>

## Reaching the contact outside the window

Outside the window, the only way to reach the contact is an approved template.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    On the **WhatsApp** screen, open the contact's conversation on the official channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Template">
    In the **Outside the 24h window** notice, click **Send Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the template">
    The **Send Template** window shows only the approved templates for that channel. Use **Search template by name...** to find the one you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in and send">
    Fill in the requested fields, such as **Fill in the variables:**, and click **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the contact replies to the template, the window opens again. The notice disappears and the message field works again.

The full walkthrough, with variables, header and buttons, is in [Official WhatsApp templates](/en/atendimento/templates-whatsapp).

<Tip>
  To reach many contacts at once outside the window, use a broadcast with a template. See [Broadcasts](/en/marketing/disparos).
</Tip>

<Note>
  No approved template yet? The **Send Template** window shows **No approved template found.** Create one in **Connections**, on the **Official WhatsApp (Meta)** card, **Templates** tab, and wait for Meta's approval. If you have access to the templates screen, the **Create template** button at the bottom of the **Send Template** window takes you straight to template creation.
</Note>

## The AI outside the window

If the AI Agent is triggered in an Official WhatsApp (Meta) conversation with the window closed, it does not reply. The conversation is left for a person on your team. In the AI Agent metrics, this case appears with the reason **Outside the 24h window**.

See more in [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia).

## What about Instagram?

Instagram has a similar rule, with some differences:

| | Official WhatsApp (Meta) | Instagram |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time limit** | 24 hours since the contact's last message | 24 hours since the person's last Direct message |
| **After the limit** | Only an approved template can be sent | There are no templates. The person needs to send a Direct message again |
| **Countdown** | None | Shows **Instagram window closes in** with the hours and minutes left |

On Instagram, a comment on a post or live does not open this window. See the details in [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram).

## Common errors

| What appears | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Message field locked and the **Outside the 24h window** notice | 24 hours have passed since the contact's last message | Send a template or wait for the contact to reply |
| Message with an alert icon and the reason **Faz mais de 24h desde a última resposta do cliente. Envie um modelo aprovado para reabrir a conversa.** | Meta refused the message because the window had already closed | Send an approved template |
| **No approved template found.** | The channel has no approved template | Create a template and wait for Meta's approval |
| **Template não entregue** with **Template variable not filled in** | A variable was left without a value | Fill in every field and send again |

<Note>
  Some of these notices appear in Portuguese even when the CRM is set to English, such as **Template não entregue** and the reason for a message refused outside the window.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I type in the conversation?">
    The conversation is outside the 24-hour window. The contact has not sent a message for more than 24 hours, or has never sent one on this channel. Send a template with the **Send Template** button or wait for the contact to reply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I sent several messages. Why did the window close anyway?">
    Only the contact's message opens the window. Messages sent by you or your team do not count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The notice does not show the Send Template button. What do I do?">
    On some official channels the notice does not have this button. Wait for the contact to reply so the window opens again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I sent a template. Can I send free-form text now?">
    Not yet. The window opens when the contact replies. After that, you can send text, audio and files for 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does WhatsApp (QR Code) have this rule too?">
    No. The 24-hour window exists only on Official WhatsApp (Meta).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
