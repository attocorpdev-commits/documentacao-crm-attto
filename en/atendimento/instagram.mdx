The **Instagram** screen brings together the conversations from the Instagram accounts connected to the CRM. You reply to messages, send files and update the lead in the Pipeline without opening the Instagram app.

<Note>
  <Badge color="blue" shape="pill" icon="layer-group">Prime</Badge> <Badge color="purple" shape="pill" icon="layer-group">Ultra</Badge>

  Available on the [**Prime** and **Ultra**](/en/primeiros-passos/planos) plans. See [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).
</Note>

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **Support** category and click **Instagram**.

The **Instagram Direct** screen has three parts: the conversation list on the left, the open conversation in the center and the **Profile** panel on the right.

<Note>
  To receive messages, the Instagram account must be connected. See how in [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) or open [Connections in the CRM](https://app.attocorp.com/app/connections/instagram) to connect now.
</Note>

<Card title="Open Instagram in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/instagram" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your CRM account.
</Card>

## Conversation list

Pinned conversations show first. Then come the conversations with the most recent activity. A conversation with a typed message that was not sent moves up the list and shows the **Draft:** label.

When new conversations arrive while you are scrolling the list, a notice like **1 new conversation** appears. Click it to go back to the top.

### Search and filter

- **Search conversations...**: finds conversations by contact name, username, message text or date, such as `hoje` or `08/09/2026`.
- **Show unread only**: click the bell icon to see only conversations with unread messages. Click again to see all of them.
- **All channels**: if more than one account is connected, choose a channel to see only its conversations. Use **Search channel...** to find it by name.

<Tip>
  During a search, the CRM looks in every channel you can see, even if a channel is selected.
</Tip>

The search also finds older conversations that have not shown up in the list yet. They come with the contact's photo and a preview of the last message, and open with the full message history. This works for anyone on the team who has access to that conversation's channel.

### DMs and Comments tabs

The list has the **DMs** and **Comments** tabs. In the **Comments** tab, conversations are sorted by the most recent comment. A new comment gets its own unread mark, separate from the Direct one, with a sound and a notification. Live comments arrive without a sound.

If the open conversation has nothing in the current tab, use **See this person's comments** or **Go to DMs** to switch tabs.

### Pin a conversation

Hover over the contact's photo and click **Pin conversation**. To undo, click **Unpin**.

### New message alerts

At the top of the list, the bell icon shows **Mute notifications** or **Enable notifications**. Click it to turn alerts on or off.

## Send messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Click the conversation in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Type in the **Type a message...** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press **Enter** or click the send button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Send an image or file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the attachments menu">
    Click the **+** button (**Attachments and actions**) next to the message field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Click **Attach image** or **Attach file** and select the file on your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    The file name and size appear above the message field. Click the send button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The size limits are 5 MB for images, 16 MB for video and audio, and 95 MB for documents.

### Other actions

- **Quick reply**: shows in the **+** menu when your company already has saved replies. Choose a reply and it goes into the message field for you to review. See [Quick replies](/en/atendimento/respostas-rapidas).
- **Fix with AI**: corrects the text you typed. Review it before sending.
- **Send link button**: shows on some channels. Fill in **Text message**, **Button title** and **Link URL** and click **Send**.

<Note>
  On Instagram, the `/` shortcut does not open the quick replies list. Use the **+** menu.
</Note>

### Insert emoji

Click **Insert emoji**, next to the message field, to open the full emoji list, with search, categories, **Recently used** and **Skin tone**. On Instagram, the button also appears on phones.

### Instagram 24-hour window

Instagram only allows direct messages up to 24 hours after the person's last Direct message. A comment does not open this window. Near the end, the conversation shows how much time is left, such as **Instagram window closes in**.

After the window closes, the conversation shows a notice above the message field. If the person commented recently, click **Reply in the comment** to answer publicly. You can also click **Try sending anyway**: Instagram decides and, if it refuses, the message shows the reason.

## Message types you receive

Besides text, images, video and audio, the conversation shows notices when the contact interacts with your profile:

| Notice in the conversation | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Mentioned you in a story** | The contact tagged your profile in a story |
| **Reply to your story** | The contact replied to one of your stories |
| **Shared a post** | The contact sent a post |
| **Shared a reel** | The contact sent a reel |
| **Comment on the live** | The contact commented on a live stream |

On images, hover and click **Zoom in** or **Download**. On shared content, click **View content on Instagram** to open a preview.

### Reply to a comment

When the message is a comment, the **Reply to comment** link shows below it. This option is available on some channels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reply">
    Click **Reply to comment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    Type in the **Reply to comment...** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press **Enter**. The CRM confirms with the **Reply sent!** notice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn the AI on and off

<Warning>
  The integrated AI only handles WhatsApp conversations. It does not reply to Instagram conversations. Here, you reply through the screen, or the Custom AI does, if your company uses this feature.
</Warning>

The Custom AI controls are at the top of the conversation. They show depending on your plan and your permission. Hover over each icon to see what it does.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Disable Custom AI in this conversation** | Turns off the Custom AI in this conversation only |
| **Enable Custom AI in this conversation** | Turns the Custom AI back on in this conversation |

<Warning>
  The AI icons at the top of the list apply to the whole company, not only to the open conversation. One of them turns the Custom AI on and off in all Instagram conversations. The other turns the integrated AI on and off in all of the company's channels, including WhatsApp ones. Use them with care.
</Warning>

### What changes on the AI Agent screen

Instagram accounts do not show up to turn on the AI on the [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) screen. And Instagram conversations are not in the **Pick conversations** list, in the **Turn the AI on or off per conversation** section. Where you might expect the AI, the CRM shows the notice **The integrated AI only handles WhatsApp conversations.**

The **Turn the AI off for all conversations** option and turning on the AI in a single conversation only apply to WhatsApp conversations. See [WhatsApp](/en/atendimento/whatsapp).

<Note>
  The Instagram conversation shows no notice or button to turn on the integrated AI in a single conversation.
</Note>

## Contact panel

Click the contact's name or photo, or the **View profile** button, to open the **Profile** panel.

| Field | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy profile link** | Copies the address of the contact's Instagram profile |
| **Create activity** | Creates an activity linked to this contact |
| **Pipeline** | Chooses which Pipeline the lead is in |
| **Stage** | Chooses the lead's stage in the Pipeline |
| **Tags** | Click **Add tag...** to search for or create a tag |
| **Value (R$)** | Deal value |
| **Note** | Notes about the lead |
| **History** | Shows **First contact**, **Last message** and the **Total** number of messages |
| **Media, links and docs** | Opens everything sent and received in the conversation, in the **Media**, **Links** and **Docs** tabs. Click **View in chat** to jump to the message |

After filling in **Value (R$)** and **Note**, click **Save**.

## Delete a contact

The **Apagar contato** (delete contact) trash icon, at the top of the conversation, deletes the conversation, the media and the contact from the CRM. In the **Apagar contato** window, type `CONFIRMAR` and click **Apagar contato**. This cannot be undone and applies to the CRM only: on Instagram the messages stay as they are.

The button appears for the account owner and for managers. Other team members need the **Apagar contato** permission, on the [Team](/en/conta/equipe) screen. This window is shown in Portuguese in every language. See more in [WhatsApp](/en/atendimento/whatsapp#delete-a-contact).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The screen says I have no Instagram channel assigned. What should I do?">
    Your user does not have access to any Instagram account yet. Ask the person in charge of your company's account to give you access on the [Team](/en/conta/equipe) screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The list says there are no Instagram conversations yet. Is that normal?">
    Yes, when the account was just connected or has not received messages yet. Check the connection in [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message turned red with the notice 'Another app connected to Instagram controls this conversation'. What should I do?">
    Another tool linked to your Instagram (ManyChat, Kommo, n8n or comment automation) took over the conversation and Meta rejects the CRM's reply. The fix is a one-time setting in Meta. See the step by step in [Instagram: messages rejected by another app](/en/atendimento/instagram-mensagens-recusadas).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does it show image, video or audio unavailable?">
    The CRM could not load this content. To see it, open the conversation directly on Instagram.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the quick reply option in the attachments menu. Why?">
    It only shows when there is at least one saved reply. Create the first one in [Quick replies](/en/atendimento/respostas-rapidas).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The AI does not reply to Instagram conversations. Is that right?">
    Yes. The integrated AI only handles WhatsApp conversations. On Instagram, you reply through the screen, or use the Custom AI, if your company has this feature.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I found the conversation through search, but I don't recognize the contact. How do I check?">
    A conversation found through search opens with the contact's photo and the full message history. Use **Copy profile link** in the **Profile** panel to open the profile on Instagram.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
