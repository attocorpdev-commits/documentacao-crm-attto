Your status tells the team and the CRM whether you are available to handle conversations. It decides whether you receive new conversations through automatic distribution.

## The three statuses

| Status | Color | Receives new conversations from automatic distribution? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Online** | Green | Yes, if you take part in the support queue. |
| **Away** | Yellow | No. |
| **Offline** | Gray | No. |

The colored dot on your photo shows your current status. The same dot appears next to your name for the team, for example in the **Configurar Fila** window and in the **Team now** table of the [Manager Area](/en/gestao/area-gestor).

## Your status changes on its own

- When you open the CRM, your status becomes **Online**.
- When you close the CRM tab or sign out, your status becomes **Offline**.
- If the browser closes without notifying the CRM, you stop receiving new conversations after 10 minutes.

<Warning>
  If you chose **Away** and then closed and reopened the CRM, your status goes back to **Online**. Check the dot on your photo every time you sign in.
</Warning>

## Change your status

<Steps>
  <Step title="From the profile menu">
    Click your photo or initial, in the top right corner, and choose **Online**, **Away** or **Offline** under **Status**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From preferences">
    In **My Profile**, open **Preferences** and choose under **Status**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The change applies right away to conversation distribution.

## Inactivity warning

The warning appears only for team members. The company owner does not get it.

After 25 minutes without using the CRM, the **Are you still there?** window opens, with the text **In 2 minutes your status changes to Away and you stop receiving new conversations.** A countdown shows the time left **until you're marked as away**.

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Continue** | The window closes, you stay **Online** and the 25 minutes start over. |
| **Set me as away** | Your status changes to **Away** right away and the window closes. |

<Tip>
  For the CRM, using it means clicking, typing, scrolling or tapping the screen. Just moving the mouse does not count.
</Tip>

<Note>
  If you are already **Away** or **Offline**, the warning does not appear.
</Note>

### When the countdown ends

If nobody answers within 2 minutes, your status changes to **Away**. The window then shows **Welcome back**, with the time you went away, and tells you that you did not receive new conversations from the queue in the meantime.

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Resume work** | Your status goes back to **Online**. |
| **Stay as away** | The window closes and you stay **Away**. |

The countdown follows the clock, even with the CRM in another tab or the computer asleep. If the time ran out while you were gone, you see **Welcome back** as soon as you return.

<Tip>
  The Esc key also answers the window. During the countdown, it works as **Continue**. After you went away, it works as **Stay as away**.
</Tip>

## What your status changes in customer service

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A new conversation arrives and you are **Online** | You may receive it through automatic distribution. See [Queue and round-robin](/en/atendimento/fila-rodizio). |
| A new conversation arrives and you are **Away** or **Offline** | It goes to another available person or goes on hold. |
| You go back **Online** and there are conversations on hold | The conversation that has been waiting the longest may go to you. |
| Conversations that are already yours | Stay with you, whatever your status. |
| Assignment from the Pipeline | People who are **Away** or **Offline** cannot be chosen in **Conversation owner**. |
| Assignment from the **WhatsApp** screen | Anyone in the **Team** list can be chosen, even if away. |

Your status does not change your notifications. Bell and push alerts follow the choices you made in the bell's **Preferences** tab. See [Profile](/en/conta/perfil).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm going to lunch. Do I need to change my status?">
    It is the safest choice. Pick **Away** before you leave, so you do not receive new conversations while you are gone. If you forget, the inactivity warning does it for you after 25 minutes plus the 2-minute countdown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm Online, but I'm not getting new conversations. Why?">
    Check whether **Participates in the support queue** is checked in your permissions, whether you have access to the channel and, with **Fila automática** on, whether you are on the list with **Ativo na fila** on. The owner or a manager can change this. See [Queue and round-robin](/en/atendimento/fila-rodizio).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Are you still there? warning never shows up for me.">
    It does not show up for the company owner. It also does not show up if you are already **Away** or **Offline**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does going away take my conversations?">
    No. You remain the owner of the conversations that are already yours. You only stop receiving new conversations through automatic distribution.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
