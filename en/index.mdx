The Atto Corp CRM brings together in one place customer service on WhatsApp and Instagram, the sales funnel and the artificial intelligence that replies to your leads.

This help center shows, screen by screen, how to use each part of the system.

<Tip>
  Looking for something specific? Use the search at the top of the page or press **Ctrl + K** (on Mac, **⌘ + K**) and type what you want to do, like "connect WhatsApp" or "24-hour window".
</Tip>
