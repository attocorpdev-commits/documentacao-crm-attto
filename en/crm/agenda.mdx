The Calendar shows your company's appointments in a monthly view. Here you create events, block time slots and register the services you offer.

<Note>
  The full calendar is available on all plans. Having the AI book appointments on its own, right from the conversation with the lead, is part of the Prime and Ultra plans.
</Note>

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **CRM** category and click **Calendar**.

The screen has two tabs: **Calendar** and **Services**.

<Card title="Open Calendar in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/calendar" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Move around the calendar

- Use **Previous** and **Next** to change the month, or pick the month and year from the lists.
- Each day shows up to two appointments. When there are more, a counter appears.
- Type in **Search events...** to filter by title, description or location.
- Click **Refresh** to reload the appointments.

## Create an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **New** or click straight on the day you want in the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date">
    In the calendar of the **New Event** window, click the day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    The **Event title** is required. In **Time**, enter the start and end. The **Description** and **Location** fields are optional and are only kept when Google Calendar is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create event**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For an appointment with no set time, turn on **All day**.

If you have services registered, the **Service** field appears. When you choose one, the service name is added to the event title.

<Note>
  If there is already an appointment at the same time, you see the notice **Time slot already taken**. Choose another time.
</Note>

## Edit or cancel an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the appointment">
    Click the appointment in the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change or delete">
    To change it, adjust the details and click **Save changes**. To cancel the appointment, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The **Delete** button removes the appointment immediately, without asking for confirmation.
</Warning>

## Block time off

Use time off to reserve slots when you are not available.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open time off">
    Click **Time off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date">
    In the **Block Time Slots (Time off)** window, enter the **Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the time slots">
    Click the time slots you want to block. To close the whole day, click **Block full day**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Block**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Days with time off are highlighted in red in the calendar. To free up a slot, click the block and then **Delete**.

## Connect Google Calendar

With your Google account connected, the events in your Google Calendar appear in the Calendar. The appointments you create here are also sent to Google.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Click **Connect Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Sign in with your Google account and allow access to your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    You return to the CRM and see the notice **Google Calendar connected!**. The account appears in **Connected as**, below the screen title.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After connecting, the new event window gets the **Guests** field. Enter the emails separated by commas. Each person receives the invite. Turn on **Google Meet** so the event comes with a meeting link.

To use another account, click **Change account**. To turn off the integration, click **Disconnect Google**.

<Note>
  Even without Google connected, the Calendar works. In that case the CRM keeps the title, date and time. Description and location are only kept in events sent to Google.
</Note>

## Services

On the **Services** tab you register what your company offers, with professional, price and duration.

### Register a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **New Service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    The **Service Name** and **Professional** fields are required. **Price (R$)** and **Description** are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set whether it is active">
    Keep **Active service** on for the service to appear when creating appointments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit or delete a service

On the service card, click **Edit** to change the details. To remove it, click the trash icon and confirm.

Use **Search services...** to find a service by name or by professional.

### Set the service duration

Click **Default Time**, choose the duration and click **Apply to All**. The options range from 30 minutes to 2 hours.

<Warning>
  The duration is the same for all registered services. When you change it, all of them get the new time.
</Warning>

## Clear the month's calendar

The **Clear** button removes the appointments you created in the month open on the screen. Appointments created by the AI or by coworkers stay. Events that come from Google Calendar are not deleted.

<Warning>
  This action cannot be undone. Check the month shown before confirming.
</Warning>

## Event reminders on WhatsApp

To remind people about an event, such as a class, a service or a meeting, use the **Event reminders** screen. The CRM sends on its own, on WhatsApp, the attendance confirmation, the reminder and the notice that the event is about to start, up to 30 minutes before.

In the side menu, open **Marketing** and click **Event reminders**, right below **Collections**. The screen lists your events, with the status, the date and the next reminder.

<Card title="Open Event reminders in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/avisos">
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your CRM account.
</Card>

<Note>
  Inside the **Event reminders** screen, buttons and messages are shown in Portuguese in every language. In this section, the Portuguese label comes first, with the English meaning in parentheses.
</Note>

<Note>
  Event reminders are separate from Calendar appointments. Anyone with access to **Collections** can use the screen. To give someone on your team access, check the **Collections** page in their permissions. See [Team](/en/conta/equipe).
</Note>

### Create a reminder

Click **Novo aviso** (new reminder). Setup has 4 steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Event">
    Fill in **Nome do evento** (event name), the date and the time (Brasília time). **Local (opcional)** (location, optional) and **Link (opcional)** (link, optional) only go into the message when filled in. Under **Por qual WhatsApp os avisos vão sair?** (which WhatsApp will send the reminders?), pick the channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="People">
    Upload the spreadsheet with the name and phone of who will receive them, or click **Escolher dos contatos** (pick from contacts) to select people from your list. You can use both together: the same phone goes in only once. Rows with a problem show in red for you to fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reminders">
    Turn on the reminders you want to send and adjust the message of each one. Each reminder already comes with a ready-made text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    See when each reminder goes out and to how many people. Under **Seu WhatsApp** (your WhatsApp), send yourself a test. Then click **Ligar avisos** (turn on reminders). If you prefer to leave it for later, click **Salvar sem ligar** (save without turning on).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When each reminder goes out

| Reminder | When it goes out |
| --- | --- |
| **7 dias antes**, **5 dias antes**, **3 dias antes**, **2 dias antes** (7, 5, 3, 2 days before) | On the chosen day, within the sending hours |
| **1 dia antes (véspera)** (1 day before, the eve) | On the eve, within the sending hours |
| **No dia do evento** (on the event day) | On the day, within the sending hours |
| **Algumas horas antes** (a few hours before) | From 1 to 12 hours before the event, at the exact time |
| **30 minutos antes** (30 minutes before) | 30 minutes before the event, at the exact time |

Day reminders go out within **Avisos por dia saem entre** (day reminders go out between), which starts as 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Hour reminders go out between 7 a.m. and 10 p.m. A reminder whose time has already passed, or that would fall after the event, does not go out.

### Messages

Each reminder has a ready-made template: **Confirmação** (confirmation), **Lembrete** (reminder) or **Começa em 30 minutos** (starts in 30 minutes). Use **Inserir campo:** (insert field) to add each person's and the event's data. The **Como a pessoa vai ver** (how the person will see it) preview shows the result.

| Field | What goes in |
| --- | --- |
| `{nome}` | The person's first name |
| `{evento}` | Event name |
| `{data}` | Event day |
| `{hora}` | Event time |
| `{local}` | Address or place of the event |
| `{link}` | Event link, such as a livestream or sign-up |

<Note>
  On Official WhatsApp (Meta), each message needs to be approved first. Click **Enviar para aprovação** (send for approval). It is usually approved in minutes. The reminders can only be turned on after all messages are approved.
</Note>

<Warning>
  On WhatsApp (QR Code), the schedule preview shows sending risks in red, such as pace and the daily limit. The daily limit is shared with [Collections](/en/marketing/cobrancas): reminders only use what is left.
</Warning>

People who asked not to receive messages are left out of the reminders.

### Track and change a reminder

Click an event in the list to see the status of each reminder and each person: sent, queued, not delivered and not sent, with the reason. Use **Buscar por nome ou telefone** (search by name or phone) to find someone.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Editar** (edit) | Changes the people, the reminders and the messages |
| **Pausar** (pause) or **Ligar** (turn on) | Pauses or turns the reminders back on. While paused nothing goes out, and a reminder whose time passes in the meantime does not go out later |
| **Remarcar** (reschedule) | Changes the event date and time. The reminders go out again, counting from the new date |
| **Cancelar evento** (cancel event) | Stops all reminders and cancels what is queued. It cannot be undone |
| **Tentar de novo** (try again) | Resends the messages that failed, for everyone or for one person |
| **Tirar da lista** (remove from list) | Removes a person from the event. They no longer receive the reminders |

<Tip>
  To change the date of a reminder that is on, use **Remarcar** (reschedule), not **Editar** (edit).
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Google Calendar to use the Calendar?">
    No. The connection is optional. Without it, appointments are stored only in the CRM.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I scheduled an activity in Contacts. Does it show up here?">
    Yes. What you schedule with the **Schedule activity** button, inside a contact, appears in the Calendar on the chosen day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the Service field when creating an appointment. Why?">
    The field only appears when there is at least one active service registered on the **Services** tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the AI book appointments on its own?">
    Yes, on the Prime and Ultra plans. Appointments booked by the AI appear in the Calendar along with the others. See the plans at [attocorp.com](https://attocorp.com/#planos) or open your [Subscription in the CRM](https://app.attocorp.com/app/profile/assinatura).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
