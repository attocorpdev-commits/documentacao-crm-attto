The Calendar shows your company's appointments in a monthly view. Here you create events, block time slots and register the services you offer.

<Note>
  The full calendar is available on all plans. Having the AI book appointments on its own, right from the conversation with the lead, is part of the Prime and Ultra plans.
</Note>

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **CRM** category and click **Calendar**.

The screen has two tabs: **Calendar** and **Services**.

<Card title="Open Calendar in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/calendar" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Move around the calendar

- Use **Previous** and **Next** to change the month, or pick the month and year from the lists.
- Each day shows up to two appointments. When there are more, a counter appears.
- Type in **Search events...** to filter by title, description or location.
- Click **Refresh** to reload the appointments.

## Create an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **New** or click straight on the day you want in the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date">
    In the calendar of the **New Event** window, click the day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    The **Event title** is required. In **Time**, enter the start and end. The **Description** and **Location** fields are optional and are only kept when Google Calendar is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create event**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For an appointment with no set time, turn on **All day**.

If you have services registered, the **Service** field appears. When you choose one, the service name is added to the event title.

<Note>
  If there is already an appointment at the same time, you see the notice **Time slot already taken**. Choose another time.
</Note>

## Edit or cancel an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the appointment">
    Click the appointment in the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change or delete">
    To change it, adjust the details and click **Save changes**. To cancel the appointment, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The **Delete** button removes the appointment immediately, without asking for confirmation.
</Warning>

## Block time off

Use time off to reserve slots when you are not available.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open time off">
    Click **Time off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date">
    In the **Block Time Slots (Time off)** window, enter the **Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the time slots">
    Click the time slots you want to block. To close the whole day, click **Block full day**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Block**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Days with time off are highlighted in red in the calendar. To free up a slot, click the block and then **Delete**.

## Connect Google Calendar

With your Google account connected, the events in your Google Calendar appear in the Calendar. The appointments you create here are also sent to Google.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Click **Connect Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Sign in with your Google account and allow access to your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    You return to the CRM and see the notice **Google Calendar connected!**. The account appears in **Connected as**, below the screen title.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After connecting, the new event window gets the **Guests** field. Enter the emails separated by commas. Each person receives the invite.

To use another account, click **Change account**. To turn off the integration, click **Disconnect Google**.

<Note>
  Even without Google connected, the Calendar works. In that case the CRM keeps the title, date and time. Description and location are only kept in events sent to Google.
</Note>

## Services

On the **Services** tab you register what your company offers, with professional, price and duration.

### Register a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **New Service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    The **Service Name** and **Professional** fields are required. **Price (R$)** and **Description** are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set whether it is active">
    Keep **Active service** on for the service to appear when creating appointments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit or delete a service

On the service card, click **Edit** to change the details. To remove it, click the trash icon and confirm.

Use **Search services...** to find a service by name or by professional.

### Set the service duration

Click **Default Time**, choose the duration and click **Apply to All**. The options range from 30 minutes to 2 hours.

<Warning>
  The duration is the same for all registered services. When you change it, all of them get the new time.
</Warning>

## Clear the month's calendar

The **Clear** button removes the appointments you created in the month open on the screen. Appointments created by the AI or by coworkers stay. Events that come from Google Calendar are not deleted.

<Warning>
  This action cannot be undone. Check the month shown before confirming.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Google Calendar to use the Calendar?">
    No. The connection is optional. Without it, appointments are stored only in the CRM.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I scheduled an activity in Contacts. Does it show up here?">
    Yes. What you schedule with the **Schedule activity** button, inside a contact, appears in the Calendar on the chosen day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the Service field when creating an appointment. Why?">
    The field only appears when there is at least one active service registered on the **Services** tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the AI book appointments on its own?">
    Yes, on the Prime and Ultra plans. Appointments booked by the AI appear in the Calendar along with the others. See the plans at [attocorp.com](https://attocorp.com/#planos) or open your [Subscription in the CRM](https://app.attocorp.com/app/profile/assinatura).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
