The Pipeline is your sales funnel shown as a board. Each column is a stage of the deal and each card is a lead. You can see at a glance who just arrived, who is negotiating and who has already closed.

<Note>
  In this help center we call each column a **stage**. On screen, some buttons and fields also use the word **stage**. It is the same thing.
</Note>

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **CRM** category and click **Pipeline**.

At the top of the screen you find three numbers: **Total leads**, **Converted/Closed** and **Conversion rate**.

<Card title="Open Pipeline in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/pipeline" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your CRM account.
</Card>

## Understand the lead card

Each card shows the lead's name and phone number. When the information exists, the card also shows:

- The source channel (WhatsApp or Instagram)
- The deal value
- The interest level: **High interest**, **Medium interest** or **Low interest**
- The lead's tags (up to three, with a counter for the rest)
- The conversation summary written by the AI
- Your note
- How long ago the last interaction was
- The **Idle** label, when the lead has gone a long time without a next action
- A bell, when there is an activity scheduled for the lead. Click it to open this contact's activities

## Move a lead to another stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drag the card">
    Click the card, hold it and drop it in the column of the new stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log the next action">
    The **Do you want to create a next activity for this contact?** window opens. Choose the **Action type**, the **Assignee**, the **Date** and, if you want, the **Time (optional)** and the **Notes / context**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Create activity** to move the lead and schedule the task. If there is nothing to schedule, click **Skip / No next action**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you click **Cancel** or close the window, the lead is not moved.
</Warning>

When the destination stage is a loss stage, the **Loss reason** field is required. The options are: **Price**, **Timing**, **Competitor**, **No interest**, **No response**, **No budget** and **Other**.

<Tip>
  On a phone you cannot drag. Tap the stage name at the top to see its leads. To move one, open the card and use **Move contact**.
</Tip>

## Open a card

Click the card to see the lead's details:

| Area | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **AI summary** | Read the conversation summary |
| **Pipeline and stage** | Choose another pipeline or stage and click **Move contact** |
| **Conversation owner** | Set who handles the lead (WhatsApp leads only) |
| **Value (R$)**, **Note** and **Tags (comma-separated)** | Fill them in and click **Save** |
| **Activities** | See the contact's tasks and create another one with **New** |
| **Movement history** | See which stages the lead went through and who moved it |

At the bottom of the card, click **Open in WhatsApp** or **Open in Instagram** to go straight to the conversation.

To take a lead off the board without deleting anything, hover over the card and click the **x** (**Remove from pipeline**). The contact and the conversation stay saved.

## Create and organize stages

Click **Edit stages**, above the board.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a stage">
    In **Add new stage**, type the name, choose the color and the icon and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename or change the color">
    Click the stage's pencil (**Edit**), change the name, icon or color and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the order">
    Drag the stage by the handle next to its name or use the up and down arrows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a stage">
    Click the trash can (**Remove**), choose in **Move to** the stage that will receive the leads and confirm with **Remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The palette colors are **Gray**, **Yellow**, **Orange**, **Green**, **Red**, **Blue** and **Primary**. In **Custom color** you can pick any other color.

<Note>
  The first stage of the funnel cannot be removed, and the funnel needs at least one stage. When you create a stage, you cannot reuse the name of another one.
</Note>

## Work with more than one pipeline

Click **Pipelines** to choose which funnels show on the screen. You can select more than one: they show one below the other.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a pipeline">
    Click **Pipelines** and then **New pipeline**. Fill in the **Pipeline name**. If you want, choose a **Linked channel**. Click **Create pipeline**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit, duplicate or delete">
    In the pipeline list, hover over the name and use the **Edit**, **Duplicate** or **Delete** buttons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With a **Linked channel**, leads that arrive through that channel go automatically into the first stage of that pipeline.

The same list has two options to reorganize funnels:

- **Merge with…** combines two pipelines. You choose which destination stage each source stage goes to.
- **Split by channel…** creates a new pipeline with the same stages and moves the contacts from one channel into it, each one in the stage it was already in. Choose the **Channel** (WhatsApp or Instagram), the **Specific channel** and the **New pipeline name**. The new funnel becomes the default for that channel.

If the **No funnel** button shows, there are leads outside any pipeline. Click it to open the **Leads without a funnel** window.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the lead">
    Use the **Search by name or phone...** field. A second number saved on the lead, such as the one from a form, also works here. To see the rest of the list, click **Load more**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination">
    Click **Assign funnel** on the lead and select the **Pipeline** and the **Stage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Move to funnel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters and search

- **Search lead...** searches by name, phone number, AI summary or note. If the lead has a second number saved, such as the phone number the person left on a form, that number also works in the search.
- **Channel** shows only the leads from the selected channels.
- **Member** shows only the leads of one assignee, or those that are **Unassigned**.
- **Date** filters by the period the lead came in: **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 60 days** or **Last 90 days**. Use **Custom** to pick the dates.
- The funnel button next to it opens **Filter stages**, to hide or show columns. When the funnel has many stages, the list scrolls inside the window. At the bottom, **All** and **None** select and clear everything at once.

## Apply a tag to a whole stage

Hover over the column title and click the tag icon (**Apply tag to column**). Type the tag name or choose a suggestion. The tag is added to the ones the lead already has.

## Open the board in another window

Click **New window**, above the board. The pipeline opens on its own in a separate window. There you can move and edit leads. To open conversations or activities, use the main screen. It is useful to keep the funnel on a second monitor while you handle conversations.

## The AI moving leads

<Note>
  Available on the **Prime** and **Ultra** plans.
</Note>

The AI Agent can move the lead to another stage on its own as the conversation moves forward. This happens when the lead shows clear interest or confirms the purchase.

- The AI only moves the lead forward. It never moves the lead back to an earlier stage.
- The AI does not move the lead to another pipeline.
- When it moves a lead, the **Lead moved automatically** notice shows.
- In **Movement history**, these changes show as **System (AI)**.

The AI also keeps the card's **Value (R$)** up to date: whenever it recalculates the deal value during the conversation, the card shows the new number. The exception is a lead that came from a form with the value already filled in. In that case the form value is used, and the AI does not change it.

<Note>
  This feature depends on what is enabled on your account. If leads do not move on their own, talk to the person in charge of your company's account.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The **Reset** button deletes all of the company's leads in the Pipeline. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the buttons to create a pipeline or edit stages. Why?">
    These buttons depend on permission. Ask the account owner to enable pipeline management for your access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I removed a lead from the pipeline. Did I lose the conversation?">
    No. The contact and the conversation stay saved. The lead now shows in **No funnel** and can go back to a pipeline whenever you want.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the column show fewer cards than the number in the title?">
    Very full columns load cards a few at a time. Scroll to the end of the column to load more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to create an activity every time I move a lead?">
    No. Click **Skip / No next action** and the lead is moved without an activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
