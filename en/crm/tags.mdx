Tags are colored labels you put on leads. They help you separate groups, such as VIP customers, leads from a campaign or people who asked for a callback.

On the Tags screen you create, edit and delete labels. Applying a tag to each lead is done in the Pipeline and in Contacts.

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **CRM** category and click **Tags**.

At the top of the screen there are three numbers:

- **Total tags:** how many tags exist.
- **Uses in leads:** how many times tags have been applied.
- **With description:** how many tags have a description filled in.

Each tag appears on a card with its color, name, description and the number of leads using it.

<Card title="Open Tags in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/tags" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Create a tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **New tag**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a name">
    Fill in the **Name** field. Example: VIP Customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the color">
    In **Color**, click one of the colors. The preview at the top of the window shows how the tag will look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it, if you want">
    In **Description**, explain when to use the tag. The field is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Fill in the description. It helps the whole team use the same tag the same way.
</Tip>

## Edit a tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open editing">
    Hover over the tag card and click the pencil.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change what you need">
    Change the name, color or description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you change the name, the tag is renamed on every lead that already uses it. The window tells you how many leads will be affected.
</Note>

## Delete a tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the trash icon">
    Hover over the tag card and click the trash icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete tag** window, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  When you delete a tag, it is removed from every lead that uses it. The leads are not deleted, they only lose that label.
</Warning>

<Note>
  If you only see the contacts from your own channels, the tag is removed only from those contacts and the message **Tag removed from your contacts** appears. To delete it from everyone, ask a manager.
</Note>

## See the leads with a tag

Click the tag card. A side panel opens with:

- The count in **Linked leads**
- The tag color
- The list of leads with name, phone, date added and stage
- The **Edit** and **Delete** buttons

The list shows 20 leads per page. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between pages.

## Find a tag

Type in the **Search tags...** field. The screen shows only the tags that contain the text you typed.

Tags appear in order of use, from most used to least used. The screen shows 12 tags per page.

Click **Refresh** to reload the numbers after applying tags on another screen.

## Apply tags to leads

There are four ways to put a tag on a lead.

### On one lead, from the Pipeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    In the Pipeline, click the lead card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the tags">
    In the **Tags (comma-separated)** field, write the tags. Example: vip, callback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On one lead, from Contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In Contacts, click the lead's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the tag">
    Click **Add tag...** and choose a tag from the list. To create a new one, type the name and press Enter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a tag, click the **x** next to it.

### On several leads at once

In Contacts, select the leads you want. In the bar that appears, type the tags and click **Apply**. To remove them, click **Remove tags**.

### On all leads in a stage

In the Pipeline, hover over the column title and click the label icon (**Apply tag to column**). Choose or type the tag.

<Note>
  If you type a tag on a lead that does not exist yet, it starts showing on the Tags screen. You can then open editing to choose its color and description.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a tag delete the leads?">
    No. The leads stay in the CRM. They only lose the deleted label.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a lead have more than one tag?">
    Yes. The Pipeline card shows the first three, with a counter for the rest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I created a tag, but it shows zero leads. Is that right?">
    Yes. The tag already exists and is ready to use. The number goes up when you apply the tag to a lead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I applied the tag and the number did not change. What do I do?">
    Click **Refresh** to reload the screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
