The Activities screen is your sales to-do list. It shows who you need to talk to today, your scheduled follow-ups and what is already past due.

## What an activity is

An activity is a task linked to a lead. Example: call Maria tomorrow at 10 a.m. to confirm the proposal.

Each activity has a type, a date, an assignee and a note. The available types are:

- **Call**
- **Send message**
- **Follow up**
- **Send proposal**
- **Confirm payment**
- **Check interest**
- **Reschedule contact**
- **Route to support**
- **Other**

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **CRM** category and click **Activities**.

At the top of the screen there are four numbers:

- **My overdue:** your tasks that are past due.
- **My today:** your tasks dated today.
- **Active (visible):** pending tasks within the current filter.
- **Completed:** tasks already finished within the current filter.

Activities are grouped by due date: **Overdue**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Upcoming** and **No date**.

<Card title="Open Activities in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/activities" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Create an activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start creating">
    Click **New activity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the contact">
    In the **Choose contact** window, search by name or phone and click the lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the task">
    Choose the **Type**, the **Date** and the **Assignee**. If you want, enter the **Time (optional)** and the **Notes / context**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create activity**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you do not enter a time, the activity is set for 9 a.m. on the chosen day.
</Note>

### Other ways to create one

- **When you move a lead in the Pipeline.** Every time you move a lead to another stage, the CRM asks what the next action is. When you click **Create activity**, the task appears here.
- **Inside the lead card.** In the Pipeline, open the card and, in the **Activities** area, click **New**.

## Mark as completed

On the activity card, click the green **Complete** button. The task leaves the pending list.

To see what has already been done, change the status filter to **Completed** or **All**.

Completed it by mistake? Find the task among the completed ones and click **Reopen**.

## Edit or reschedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open editing">
    On the activity card, click the pencil (**Edit / reschedule**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the details">
    Change the **Type**, the **Assignee**, the **Date**, the **Time** or the **Note**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each card shows

Each activity shows:

- The lead's name and the task type
- The stage the lead is in on the Pipeline
- The deal value, when filled in
- The note
- The date and time, the assignee and the phone
- The conversation summary made by the AI, when there is one

Click the chat bubble (**Open conversation**) to go straight to this lead's conversation.

## Filters

Use the **Filters:** bar to find what you need.

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search contact, phone, or note...** | Searches for the text you type |
| **Active**, **Completed** or **All** | Chooses the task status |
| **All types** | Shows only one activity type |
| **All assignees** | Shows one person's tasks, or the ones with **No assignee** |
| **Mine** | Shows only the tasks where you are the assignee |

<Note>
  The assignee filter appears only for the account owner and for managers.
</Note>

When you click the bell on a Pipeline card, the Activities screen opens showing only that contact's tasks. To see everything again, click the contact's name shown in the filter bar.

## Delete an activity

On the card, click the red **Delete** button and confirm.

<Warning>
  Deleting cannot be undone. Only the account owner and managers can delete activities.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Start the day with **Overdue** and then move on to **Today**. That way no lead goes without a reply.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The screen is empty. How do I start?">
    Click **New activity** or move a lead to another stage in the Pipeline and log the next action. The task appears here right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I reschedule a completed task?">
    On the Activities screen, click **Reopen** and then edit the date. On the lead card, in the Pipeline, editing a completed task already reopens it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do the numbers at the top change when I use filters?">
    **My overdue** and **My today** always count all your tasks. **Active (visible)** and **Completed** follow the applied filter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the Idle notice on the Pipeline card mean?">
    It means the lead has gone a long time without interaction and with no next action scheduled. Create an activity so you do not lose the contact.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
