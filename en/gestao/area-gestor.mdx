The **Manager Area** shows the team's queue live: who is waiting, for how long and with whom. It helps you decide where to act first.

<Note>
  Available on the **Prime** and **Ultra** plans, and as an add-on enabled on specific accounts. It appears for the account owner. Members registered as agents do not see this item in the menu.
</Note>

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **CRM** category and click **Manager Area**.

If your company's plan does not include this feature, the screen shows the **Prime · Ultra feature** notice with the **Upgrade now** and **Talk to support** buttons. See [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).

The screen updates on its own. You do not need to reload the page.

<Card title="Open Manager Area in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/gestor" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Queue counters

At the top there are four counters. Click one to filter the list. The screen opens with **Awaiting reply** selected.

| Indicator | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **In queue** | Conversations awaiting a reply or with no agent. |
| **Awaiting reply** | Conversations where the customer spoke last and is waiting for a response. |
| **Long wait** | Conversations awaiting a reply for longer than the limit set in **Long wait (min)**. |
| **Unassigned** | Conversations with no agent in charge. |

Next to the subtitle you see the total of open conversations, for example "42 open".

<Note>
  The list loads up to 300 conversations. When there are more, you see the notice **showing the 300 oldest**.
</Note>

## Filters and sorting

| Filter | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | Click a name to see only that person's conversations. The number in parentheses is how many are waiting. Click again to remove the filter. |
| **Stage** | Click a Pipeline stage to see only the conversations in that stage. |
| **Depth** | Filter by lead temperature: **Hot**, **Warm** or **Cold**. The **Avoid** button shows only contacts that should not be approached. |
| **Sort** | Choose between **Longest wait**, **Shortest wait**, **Name** and **Stage**. |
| **Long wait (min)** | Set after how many minutes a wait counts as long. The default is 60. It accepts 5 to 1440. |

Above the list, the **Showing X of Y** line tells you how many conversations passed the filters.

<Tip>
  The filters are saved in the page address. Copy the link to share the same view with another manager, or save it to your bookmarks.
</Tip>

## How to read each conversation in the list

Each row is a conversation. Click it to open the conversation and act.

- **Name and stage**: the contact's name and the Pipeline stage they are in.
- **Robot icon**: the AI is on in this conversation.
- **Shield icon**: the contact asked not to receive messages.
- **Last message**: the customer's last message.
- **Owner**: the agent's name or **Unassigned**.
- **1 message**: the conversation has a single message.
- **Wait time**: shown on the right, for example "2h 15min". It is highlighted when the customer is waiting.
- **Highlighted number**: number of unread messages.

### Service quality signals

Below the last message you see labels that help you assess the conversation.

| Signal | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Hot**, **Warm**, **Cold** | Lead temperature, based on the interest shown and the time since their last message. |
| **Avoid** | Contact that should not be approached. Hover to see the reason, such as **opt-out**, **refused** or **already a client**. |
| **pasted number** | The team's last send was just a number, with no message. |
| **false premise** | Someone resumed a conversation idle for 30 days or more as if it were recent. |
| **greeting without name** | The greeting sent did not use the contact's name. |
| Person or robot icon | Shows whether the last send was made by a person, on the conversation screen, or automatically. |
| Delivery icon | One check for sent, two checks for delivered or read, an alert for a delivery error. |

## Team now

The **Team now** table shows each person on the team and the day's numbers. The dot next to the name shows whether the person is **online**, **away** or **offline**.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | The person's name. **Owner** marks the account owner. **in queue** marks who is receiving leads in the distribution. |
| **Open** | Open conversations with this person. |
| **Waiting** | Their conversations where the customer is waiting for a reply. |
| **Oldest** | Time of the longest wait among the waiting conversations. |
| **Sent today** | Messages sent today from the conversation screen. Automatic sends and broadcasts do not count. |
| **Received today** | Messages received today, counted for the conversation's current owner. |
| **Moved today** | Leads the person dragged to another stage in the Pipeline today. Changes made in other ways are not included. |
| **Activities** | This person's activities for the day. |

## Channels, queue and today's leads

At the bottom of the screen there are three cards.

- **Channels down**: lists channels with a problem, marked as **reconnect**, **disconnected** or **delivery error**. With no problems, you see **All connected.**
- **Queue now**: how many leads are waiting to be distributed, with their names. When empty, it shows **No one waiting in the queue.**
- **Leads received today**: leads distributed by the assignment queue today.

## How to act from the screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with long waits">
    Click **Long wait** and keep the order on **Longest wait**. Whoever has waited longest stays at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle conversations with no owner">
    Click **Unassigned** to see the conversations no one has taken. Click one of them to open the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prioritize hot leads">
    In **Depth**, click **Hot** to see first who is most likely to close.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the team">
    In **Team now**, see who has many conversations in **Waiting** and who is offline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the channels">
    If there is anything in **Channels down**, reconnect the channel in [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the Manager Area in the menu. Why?">
    The item appears for companies on the Prime and Ultra plans and is hidden for agents. It is not enabled on the Team screen. Check your plan in [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos). If your plan includes the screen and it does not appear for you, talk to support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The list went empty after filtering.">
    You see **Nothing in the queue with this filter.** when no conversation matches the filters. Click the active filters again to remove them, or select the **In queue** counter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Sent today low for an agent?">
    The column counts only the messages the person sent from the conversation screen. Automatic replies and broadcasts are left out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A notice said the queue could not be loaded.">
    Click **Try again**. If the notice continues, talk to support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
