The **Weekly Report** screen keeps an automatic summary of each week of your customer service. Every number comes with a plain explanation, and the list of customers waiting for a reply becomes a work queue.

<Note>
  <Badge color="blue" shape="pill" icon="layer-group">Prime</Badge> <Badge color="purple" shape="pill" icon="layer-group">Ultra</Badge>

  Available on the [**Prime** and **Ultra**](/en/primeiros-passos/planos) plans. It depends on the company's plan, not on an individual permission. If it doesn't show up in your menu, see [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).
</Note>

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **CRM** category and click **Weekly Report**.

<Card title="Open Weekly Report in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/relatorios" horizontal>
  Goes straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your CRM account.
</Card>

## When the report is generated

The report comes out every Monday at 7 a.m. It covers the previous week, from Monday to Sunday.

The same summary is also sent by email to the person in charge of the account.

If there is no report yet, the screen shows **No reports yet**. The first one shows up the following Monday.

## Report list

The **Weekly reports** screen shows one card for each week, from newest to oldest. Each card shows:

- the dates of the week, for example "Sep 8 to Sep 14";
- the number of new leads;
- the amount waiting for a reply, when there is one.

Click a card to open the report. To go back to the list, click **Back**.

## What's in the report

### Main numbers

| Indicator | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Money stalled** | Value of the customers who asked and are still waiting for you to reply. |
| **New leads** | People who talked to you for the first time this week. |
| **Answered** | Customers who received at least one reply from you. |
| **No reply** | Customers who wrote and got no reply. These are opportunities to win back. |

### AI conversations this week

The **AI conversations this week** chart shows how many conversations your AI handled per day. The peaks show your busiest days.

### Response rate

**Response rate** shows, out of everyone who talked to you, how many you managed to reply to. The calculation uses **Answered** divided by the sum of **Answered** and **No reply**.

The bar changes color based on the result:

- green from 70%;
- yellow between 40% and 69%;
- red below 40%.

Below the bar, the same number appears in a plain sentence, for example: "On average, for every 10 people who wrote, you replied to 8."

### Response speed

**Response speed** shows how long, on average, the customer waited for your first reply. The value appears in seconds or minutes. Without enough data, it shows "no data".

In the same block you see:

- **Conversations handled**: total conversations handled in the week.
- **Unsubscribes**: contacts who asked to stop receiving messages during the week.

### Money stalled

Lists the customers with an open amount who are waiting for you. Each row shows the name, the customer's last message, the amount and how many days it has been stalled.

Click a customer to open the conversation and win back the sale.

### The funnel moved

Shows how many customers moved to each Pipeline stage during the week. The stages with the most movement appear, up to eight.

When there are stalled leads, the **Stalled for +5 days:** block lists the stages and the number of leads in each one.

## Send the report via WhatsApp

You can send the week's summary to a WhatsApp number, yours or that of a manager.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    In the list, click the week you want to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Send via WhatsApp">
    The **Send via WhatsApp** block is at the end of the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the number">
    In the **WhatsApp number** field, type the number with the area code. The button is only available with a valid number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send via WhatsApp">
    If it works, **Report sent** appears. If **Send in progress** appears, the send was accepted and is on its way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Because of the 24-hour rule of Official WhatsApp (Meta), the number must have talked to the company in the last 24 hours. Without that, the message may not be delivered.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Sending uses your company's connected Official WhatsApp (Meta). Without this channel connected, the send does not happen. See [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) or check your channels in [Connections in the CRM](https://app.attocorp.com/app/connections/whatsapp-oficial/canais).
</Note>

<Tip>
  Start the week with the **Money stalled** list. It already shows who has an open amount and is waiting for your reply.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I generate a report right away?">
    Not from the screen. The report is created automatically, once a week.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got the Could not send notice. What now?">
    Check that the number is correct and includes the area code. Also check that this number talked to the company in the last 24 hours and that the company's Official WhatsApp (Meta) is connected. Then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a report for a week with no activity?">
    No. When the week had no activity, there is nothing to send.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see the reports?">
    Users of companies on the Prime and Ultra plans.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
