Here you can find, month by month, everything that changed in the CRM. The most recent entries come first.

<Update label="November 2026" description="Collections, MCP, new Broadcasts and safer sign-in">

**📣 Collections and Broadcasts**

- New [Collections](/en/marketing/cobrancas) screen, in the **Marketing** group: the CRM sends automatic payment reminders, with each person's amount and link.
- Reminders stop when the customer replies, and you mark who has already paid.
- [Broadcasts](/en/marketing/disparos) has a new look, on a wide screen, with the campaign built in 4 guided steps.
- Ads that lead to WhatsApp record the conversion at Meta again when the lead moves forward a stage. See [tracking](/en/marketing/rastreamento-meta).

**🔌 Integrations**

- New [MCP](/en/plataforma/mcp): connect your AI assistant to the CRM to look up and organize your data, with keys created in **Connections**.

**💬 Conversations and WhatsApp**

- A read message shows the two check marks in the conversation list, not the clock.
- The **Delivery failure on this WhatsApp channel** alert now has an X to dismiss it in one click.
- WhatsApp (QR Code) channels no longer show false delivery failure alerts.
- New picker with every emoji, with search, categories and skin tone, on WhatsApp and Instagram.
- You can now react with an emoji to WhatsApp messages.
- New template creator for Official WhatsApp (Meta), with ready-made suggestions, step-by-step help and a rules check before sending. See [templates](/en/atendimento/templates-whatsapp).
- You can create a template right from the **Send Template** window, without leaving the conversation.
- When you send a template from the conversation, the variable marked as name already comes with the contact's first name.
- Meta errors on templates show in plain language, and the list has a new **Header** column.
- The contact profile shows the conversation's **Media, links and docs**.
- The top of the conversation is cleaner, with the AI controls grouped in the **AI** menu.
- The name the team saves with **Save contact** shows across the whole CRM, even if the WhatsApp name changes.
- The **All channels** filter stays selected when you open a conversation.

**📸 Instagram**

- When Meta rejects an [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram) message, the bubble turns red right away and shows the reason, with step-by-step help to fix it.
- Outside the 24-hour window, the CRM warns you before sending and offers a shortcut to reply through the comment. See [rejected messages](/en/atendimento/instagram-mensagens-recusadas).
- New comments now have their own alert, with a dot separate from DMs and a notification.
- Sent replies no longer disappear from the screen when the conversation refreshes.

**🤖 AI Agent**

- The AI now remembers what the customer said in audio messages. In **Keep audio transcripts**, choose **Temporary (12 hours)**, **Permanent** or **Off**. See [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia).
- Saving the prompt without changing anything no longer creates a new version.

**👥 Leads and Contacts**

- You can now delete a contact from the conversation, together with its history, the lead and the AI memory.
- The contact count in [Tags](/en/crm/tags) covers the whole company.
- The stage name matches the pipeline on every screen.

**💳 Plans and Payments**

- If you are on the monthly plan, you can switch to annual, and the monthly subscription is canceled on its own after payment. See [plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).
- The annual plan can be paid by card or PIX.
- The monthly or annual choice shows again on mobile.
- The plan is only unlocked when the payment arrives with the full amount.
- The expired plan screen has a new look, with the company name highlighted.
- Buying an extra channel or an additional AI agent is up to the company owner.

**👤 Profile and Access**

- Sign-in, sign-up and password recovery now have a security check against robots. See [first access](/en/primeiros-passos/primeiro-acesso).
- The **Set your password** screen now asks for a strong password, with at least 10 characters.
- Password recovery no longer reveals whether an email has an account. See [recover your password](/en/conta/recuperar-senha).
- In **My Profile**, the **Security** block keeps the password change apart from two-step verification. See [security](/en/conta/seguranca).
- New cookie notice with a choice per category and a new Cookie Policy. See [policies](/en/empresa/politicas).

**👥 Team**

- The "Are you still there?" prompt keeps the right time even when the tab is in the background, and has a new look with **Continue** and **Set me as away** options.

**📚 Support**

- New **Service status** link, to see whether the CRM is up, in your photo menu, on the sign-in screens and on the error screen. See [support](/en/empresa/suporte).
- The support WhatsApp has a new number. See it in [support](/en/empresa/suporte).
- New, clear error screen for an address that does not exist, restricted access or a screen that fails to load. See [common problems](/en/primeiros-passos/problemas-comuns).

**🎨 Look and feel**

- The light or dark theme button always switches, without freezing and with the right icon.

**🔒 Security and stability**

- Your media now has a protected daily backup, plus accessibility improvements to the conversation buttons.
- Several screens load faster. Everything reviewed and tested, with no impact on your data.

</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="Instagram DMs and Comments, AI per contact, and two-step verification">

**🤖 AI Agent**

- New **Improve with AI** button in the **Prompt Builder**, which suggests improvements based on real conversations. See [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia).
- The prompt text now has search and line numbers.
- The **Hand off to an agent** option suggests keywords and understands words with or without accents.
- The knowledge base tab lists, edits, and deletes every block the AI uses.
- Resetting the AI memory now really works, for one contact or for everyone.
- The AI no longer drops the first sentence of its reply and sends audio as a voice note.

**💬 Conversations and WhatsApp**

- The editor and the bubble show WhatsApp formatting, such as bold and strikethrough.
- You get a bell notification when Meta rejects the delivery of a message.
- You can now delete Official WhatsApp (Meta) templates, with confirmation, and choose how many templates to see per page.

**📸 Instagram**

- [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram) now has separate **DMs** and **Comments** tabs, without mixing the two.
- Button and automation messages show up in the conversation.
- You can hide a comment and reply to it directly.

**👥 Leads and Contacts**

- Turn the AI on or off per contact, with the **Silenced** filter and bulk actions in [Contacts](/en/crm/contatos).
- A lead can have additional cards in other pipelines of the [Pipeline](/en/crm/pipeline).
- New WhatsApp or Instagram source filter in Contacts, Pipeline, and Broadcasts.
- Tag filter in the Pipeline, and contact import that recognizes more columns, with a complete template.

**📊 Management and Reports**

- The [Dashboard](/en/gestao/dashboard) counts messages on the right day, in Brasília time, and the chart now has a channel filter.

**👤 Profile and Access**

- You can now change your password directly in **My Profile**. See [profile](/en/conta/perfil).
- New **Two-step verification**, which the company can require from the whole team. See [security](/en/conta/seguranca).

**🔒 Security and stability**

- Error messages are clearer and free of technical text, and data protection was reinforced across the CRM.

</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="Global search, Manager Area, and ambassador program">

**💬 Conversations and WhatsApp**

- New global search with the Cmd+K or Ctrl+K shortcut, which also understands dates.
- Pin up to 3 messages, star, react to, and forward messages from each message's menu.
- WhatsApp-style media viewer, with zoom, reply, and download.
- **Send Template** now has search and preview, and Official WhatsApp templates have a search bar.
- Numbers with and without the 9th digit are recognized as the same person, with no duplicate conversation.

**🤖 AI Agent**

- New **See detailed usage**, with an estimate of remaining conversations. See [AI conversations](/en/ia/creditos).
- Turn the AI on or off in many conversations at once and reset the AI memory.
- When scheduling, the AI asks for the email and creates the Google Meet link. See [Calendar](/en/crm/agenda).
- Panel to see and manage what the AI learned from each document.
- Faster initial setup, with voice answers.

**📊 Management and Reports**

- New [Manager Area](/en/gestao/area-gestor), with the live service queue.
- The weekly email report now includes a chart. See [reports](/en/gestao/relatorios).

**👤 Profile and Access**

- **Agent** and **Manager** permission bundles in one click when adding a member in [Team](/en/conta/equipe).
- When you remove a member, you choose who receives their contacts.
- New unsaved changes warning before leaving a screen.

**💳 Plans and Payments**

- Change plans from your profile and reactivate your subscription with the **Reactivate subscription** button. See [plans](/en/conta/planos).
- Adding an Official WhatsApp (Meta) channel is now automatic, with no wait for manual activation.

**🤝 Ambassadors**

- New [ambassador program](/en/empresa/embaixadores), with a referral coupon at sign-up and levels and badges in **My Profile**.

**🎨 Look and feel**

- Refreshed look for the Dashboard, the side menu, and the settings screens.

**🔒 Security and stability**

- Files and media now open only through protected links, and isolation between companies was reinforced.

</Update>

<Update label="August 2026" description="Multiple AI agents and a new AI Agent">

**🤖 AI Agent**

- You can now have more than one AI agent, chosen from a gallery.
- The [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) was redesigned, with General, Personality, Documents, Channels, Advanced, Test, and Metrics tabs.
- Prompt version history, with the option to restore a previous version.
- AI usage is now counted per conversation. See [AI conversations](/en/ia/creditos).
- AI metrics show results per channel.

**💬 Conversations and WhatsApp**

- New default send window for broadcasts and scheduled messages, which postpones instead of discarding.
- The CRM warns you when a channel is disconnected or not receiving messages, with a shortcut to reconnect.
- WhatsApp groups no longer appear in your conversations.

**👥 Leads and Contacts**

- Every company starts with a default funnel, and each lead shows its source.
- The [Pipeline](/en/crm/pipeline) is the same for the whole team: moving a card shows up for everyone right away.

**📊 Management and Reports**

- The [Dashboard](/en/gestao/dashboard) lead funnel shows how many leads reached or passed each stage.

**💳 Plans and Payments**

- 7-day free trial and a new step-by-step flow after payment. See [first access](/en/primeiros-passos/primeiro-acesso).

**🎨 Look and feel**

- New version notice in a window, with an update button in the menu, and a guided tour with intro videos.

**🔒 Security and stability**

- More reliable message sending and stronger separation between companies.

</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" description="AI Agent, quick replies, Activities, and multiple languages">

**🤖 AI Agent**

- New [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia), which serves your contacts on WhatsApp based on your documents and your prompt.
- New lead alert by WhatsApp to one or more team numbers, on Official WhatsApp (Meta).

**💬 Conversations and WhatsApp**

- [Quick replies](/en/atendimento/respostas-rapidas) with variables, inserted by typing "/" in the conversation.
- Mark a conversation as unread.
- New "+" menu for attachments, with quick replies, image, file, and template.
- **Fix with AI** checks spelling and grammar before sending, and Shift+Enter adds a line break.
- Search conversations by message content and by tag, without worrying about accents.

**👥 Leads and Contacts**

- New [Activities](/en/crm/atividades) screen, with a next-action prompt when moving a card and activity creation straight from the conversation.
- Pipeline cards show the contact's tags and activity history.
- Safely merge duplicate contacts and rename tags in bulk. See [Tags](/en/crm/tags).

**📣 Broadcasts**

- Broadcast report with delivery status per contact and a shortcut to the conversation. See [Broadcasts](/en/marketing/disparos).
- Option to skip duplicates, choose the responsible salesperson, and send in batches within a time window.
- Contacts who opt out stop receiving broadcasts on Official WhatsApp (Meta).

**🎯 Tracking**

- New [Conversion Tracking](/en/marketing/rastreamento-meta) screen for ads that lead to WhatsApp, with a connection test.

**📊 Management and Reports**

- New **Weekly Report** by email, available as a module. See [reports](/en/gestao/relatorios).

**👤 Profile and Access**

- The CRM is now available in Portuguese, English, and Spanish.
- New message notifications on your phone and computer, with a choice of which companies notify you.
- Optional service queue per [Team](/en/conta/equipe) member.

**💳 Plans and Payments**

- New Start, Prime, and Ultra plans, with checkout and recurring subscription. See [plans](/en/conta/planos).

**🎨 Look and feel**

- Dark theme across the CRM, and the app updates itself, with no need to reload the page.

</Update>

<Update label="April 2026" description="CRM launch">

**🚀 Launch**

- The Atto Corp CRM is here, with WhatsApp, Instagram, Pipeline, Contacts, and Calendar in one place. See [first access](/en/primeiros-passos/primeiro-acesso).

**💬 Conversations and WhatsApp**

- Connect your WhatsApp (QR Code) from the CRM. See [connect WhatsApp](/en/primeiros-passos/conectar-whatsapp).

- Drag cards between [Pipeline](/en/crm/pipeline) stages, with colors and a total per stage.

**📣 Broadcasts**

- [Broadcasts](/en/marketing/disparos) with indicators, CRM contacts as an audience, and a button to repeat a campaign.

**👤 Profile and Access**

- Per-member permissions that change in seconds, without reloading the page. See [Team](/en/conta/equipe).
- Install the CRM on your phone's home screen, on Android and iPhone.
