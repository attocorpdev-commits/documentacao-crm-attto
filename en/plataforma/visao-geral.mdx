The Atto Corp CRM automates customer service on WhatsApp with artificial intelligence, organizes leads in the sales funnel and sends campaigns. You connect your number through WhatsApp (QR Code) or Official WhatsApp (Meta). See the difference in [WhatsApp types](/en/primeiros-passos/tipos-whatsapp).

<Card title="Open the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/auth">
  The CRM works in your browser, at app.attocorp.com.
</Card>

## Plans

The CRM has three plans.

| Plan | In one sentence |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | The AI replies for you. |
| **Prime** | The AI replies, schedules and measures. |
| **Ultra** | Adapted to your operation, with the scope defined together with the Atto team. |

For prices and the full comparison, go to [attocorp.com/#planos](https://attocorp.com/#planos). To change plans, see [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).

<Tip>
  If you never had a plan, you can try the CRM free for 7 days, on the monthly option.
</Tip>
