The CRM's AI uses a company balance. This balance is measured in **conversations**: that is how it shows in the plan, in top-ups and in notices. The **AI Credits** screen, in Settings, shows the same balance in detail, with day-by-day usage and the statement.

<Info>
  **One conversation** is one contact served by the AI within a 24-hour window. If the same contact comes back the next day, it counts as a new conversation. Several reply bubbles in the same conversation count as one.
</Info>

## Where to see the balance

| Where | Path | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI Credits** | Settings > Artificial intelligence > **AI Credits** | **Current balance**, percentage left, **Daily consumption** for the last 30 days and **Transactions** (statement). **View usage** and **Recharge** buttons. |
| **AI conversations** | My Profile > **Subscription** | **Company balance**, how many conversations are left and the percentage. **Top up conversations** button. |
| **Conversations** | AI Agent > **General** tab | **Conversations left** and, when there is history, how many days the balance will last at the current pace. **Top up**, **See detailed usage** and **View usage** buttons. |
| **Test** | AI Agent > **Test** tab | The available balance while you test the AI. |

<Card title="Open AI Credits in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/settings/credits">
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your CRM account.
</Card>

On the **AI Credits** screen, **View usage** opens the **Usage** window, with the **Used** and **Available** conversations. On the **General** tab, **See detailed usage** opens the same window, and **View usage** opens the **Metrics** tab.

### Transactions statement

In **Transactions**, each row shows where the spending or credit came from:

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **AI (agent)** | Replies from the AI Agent to your customers. |
| **Assistant** | Use of the AI Assistant. |
| **AI setup** | Generating or refining the agent's prompt. |
| **AI usage** | Other AI uses. |
| **Recharge** | Conversation pack purchased. |
| **Adjustment** | Balance correction made by Atto. |

## What uses balance and what does not

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Uses balance" icon="circle-minus">
    - Each time the **AI Agent** serves your customers.
    - Each AI reply in the **Test** tab.
    - **Generating** or **refining** the agent's prompt.
    - Conversations with the **AI Assistant**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No charge" icon="circle-check">
    - Service handled only by people on the team.
    - When the AI does not reply, including outside business hours.
    - Reading the conversation history and the agent's documents.
    - Creating your first AI during sign-up.
    - The first prompt generation for each agent, before saving a version.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  After generating a prompt, the CRM shows how much that generation used.
</Tip>

## Plan and extra packs

- The plan includes a number of conversations per month. This quota **renews every cycle and does not roll over** to the next month.
- The **extra packs** you buy **add up and do not expire**. The amount paid for a pack is not refunded.
- The balance belongs to the company, not to each user. Every WhatsApp number with AI uses the same quota.

To see what each plan includes, go to [attocorp.com/#planos](https://attocorp.com/#planos).

## Top up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the top-up">
    Click **Recharge**, **Top up** or **Top up conversations** on any of the balance screens. The **Top up conversations** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the pack">
    In **Conversation pack**, select the amount. The **Total** shows right below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Buy**. The payment page opens in the same tab. The top-up is a one-time charge, by **credit card** or **PIX**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your account has more than one company, the window may ask for the **Company receiving the package**. The conversations you buy go into the balance of the chosen company. In this case, only the company owner can buy packs.
</Note>

Questions about the purchase? In the window itself, click **Questions? Contact support**.

## Balance notices

Inside the AI Agent, the CRM warns you as the balance goes down:

| When | Notice |
| --- | --- |
| Half of the quota used | **You've already used half of your conversations** |
| 20% or 50 conversations left | **Your conversations are running out** |
| 5% or 20 conversations left | **Your conversations are almost over** |
| 1% or 5 conversations left | **Your AI is about to stop** |
| Balance at zero | **Your conversations ran out** |

If you close a notice, it only comes back when the balance drops to the next level. After a top-up, the notices start over.

In the **AI Assistant**, the notice is **Low credit balance**, with the **Top up credits** button.

<Tip>
  Turn on **AI conversations** notifications to get alerts about your balance. See how in [Profile](/en/conta/perfil).
</Tip>

## When the balance runs out

<Warning>
  With no balance, the AI Agent **stops replying** to customers until you top up.
</Warning>

- The conversation waits for an agent from the team. When the balance comes back, the AI starts serving again.
- The company owner gets the **Your conversations ran out** notification.
- The AI Assistant, the **Test** tab and prompt generation are also blocked until the top-up.
- The CRM and service by the team keep working as normal.

## Track usage

In AI Agent > **Metrics** tab, or through the **View usage** button on the **General** tab, the **Your AI's performance** screen shows what the AI did in the **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**.

The **Spend in the period** block breaks usage down in reais:

- **Total charged**: everything the AI used in the period.
- **Delivered to the customer**: what turned into a sent reply.
- **Not delivered**: what used balance without reaching the customer, for example a repeated reply that was avoided or a sending failure. Ideally, this stays close to zero.

<Note>
  The amounts in reais on the **Metrics** screen are an estimate, so you can see where the usage went. The account balance is still counted in conversations.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The AI stopped replying. Is it a lack of balance?">
    It could be. Check **Conversations left** in AI Agent > **General** tab. If you see **Your conversations ran out**, top up. If there is still balance, see the other reasons in [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I bought a pack. Does it expire at the end of the month?">
    No. Extra packs add up and do not expire. Only the plan's monthly quota renews every cycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does testing the AI use balance?">
    Yes. Each reply in the **Test** tab uses balance, just like in real customer service.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do team members have their own balance?">
    No. The balance belongs to the company. In [Team](/en/conta/equipe), the **Share AI credits** option sets whether the member uses the company's credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I paid for the top-up and the balance did not change.">
    Payment confirmation can take a few moments. If the balance does not update within a few minutes, contact [support](/en/empresa/suporte) with the email used for the purchase. Do not pay again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
