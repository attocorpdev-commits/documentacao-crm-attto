When the AI transfers the conversation to an agent, the conversation gets a unique code, the **service protocol**. It helps the team and the customer talk about the same conversation.

<Info>
  The company owner and managers can see this setting. Only the owner can change it.
</Info>

## When the protocol is created

- When the AI hands the conversation to a person on the team.
- When someone clicks **Take over** in a conversation, on [WhatsApp](/en/atendimento/whatsapp) or on [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram).

The code appears at the top of the conversation, next to the contact's name, with a button to copy it.

## Send the protocol to the customer

The protocol is always created. You choose whether the customer also receives a message with the code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Click your photo or initial, in the top right corner, and choose **Settings**. In the **Artificial intelligence** group, click **Service protocol**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on sending">
    Turn on the **Send the protocol to the customer** switch. The message is only sent when a new protocol is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    In the **Message** field, write the text. Use `{protocolo}` where the code should appear. The default text is `Seu protocolo de atendimento é {protocolo}.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and save">
    The **Preview** shows how the message looks with a sample code. Click **Save**. The CRM confirms with **Protocol saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Open Service protocol in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/settings/protocol">
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

<Tip>
  The message accepts up to 500 characters. The counter is below the field.
</Tip>

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Write the message the customer will receive.** | The field is empty. Write the text or turn off sending. |
| **Include** `{protocolo}` **where the code should appear.** | Put `{protocolo}` in the text. |
| **Use up to 500 characters.** | Shorten the message. |
| **Only the company owner can change this setting.** | You are viewing it in read-only mode. Ask the company owner. |
| **No permission to change this company's protocol.** | Your account cannot save. Ask the company owner. |
| **Could not load the protocol settings.** | Click **Try again**. |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I turned off sending. Does the protocol stop existing?">
    No. The code is still created and appears at the top of the conversation. Turning it off only stops the customer from receiving the message.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see Service protocol in the settings.">
    The option only appears for the company owner and managers. See [Team](/en/conta/equipe).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the customer get a message on every transfer?">
    The message is only sent when a new protocol is created.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
