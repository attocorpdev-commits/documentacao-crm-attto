The AI Assistant is a chat built into the CRM. It talks to **you**. You ask about your operation's data or request a task, and it answers right away.

<Note>
  Do not confuse it with the [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia). The AI Assistant talks to you, the person using the CRM. The AI Agent talks to your leads, on WhatsApp.
</Note>

## How to access

In the side menu, open the **Main** category and click **AI Assistant**.

The screen opens with the title **AI Assistant** and the caption **Ask about data or give commands**.

<Card title="Open AI Assistant in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/assistant" horizontal>
  Takes you straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your account in the CRM.
</Card>

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your question">
    Type in the **Type your question or command...** field. If you prefer, click one of the suggestions on the screen to fill in the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press Enter or click the send button. To add a new line without sending, use Shift + Enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    The answer appears in the conversation. You can keep asking right after.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Suggestions shown on the screen

When the conversation is empty, the screen shows **How can I help?** and these suggestions:

- "Give me a full report on the operation"
- "How many leads reached out to me today?"
- "How many chats are on human support?"
- "How many interested leads do I have?"
- "What are my next appointments?"
- "Schedule a meeting tomorrow at 10am"

## What it can do

Besides answering questions about your data, the AI Assistant can carry out some tasks:

- Create an appointment in the [Calendar](/en/crm/agenda).
- Update settings.
- Create a product, with or without a photo.
- Show a product's photos.

<Note>
  To pause or turn on the AI that serves your leads, use the [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) screen. The AI Assistant does not make this change.
</Note>

<Warning>
  When the request changes something in your CRM, the **Confirm change** window appears. Nothing changes until you click **Apply**. To give up, click **Cancel**.
</Warning>

When creating a product, the assistant asks whether you want to add a photo. Choose **Upload photo** or **Create without photo**.

## Talk instead of typing

Click the microphone button, next to the text field, and say your question. The field shows **Listening...** while it captures your voice.

<Note>
  The browser needs permission to use the microphone. If you see **Speech recognition blocked**, allow the microphone in your browser settings.
</Note>

## Listen to the answers

At the top of the screen, you control the assistant's voice:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Voice on** / **Voice muted** | Turns reading the answers out loud on or off. |
| **Stop speech** | Stops the reading. It only appears while the assistant is speaking. |
| **Voice** | Opens **Select voice**, to choose the voice used for reading. |

## Organize into several conversations

Above the chat are the conversation tabs, with names like **Conversation 1**.

- Click **New** to open another conversation.
- Click a tab to go back to it.
- Click the **X** on a tab to close it.
- Click **Clear**, at the top, to erase the messages in the current conversation.

## Balance

The AI Assistant uses your account's AI balance. When the balance is low, the screen shows a notice with the **Top up credits** button. If the balance runs out, the conversation shows a notice that takes you to the top-up. See [AI conversations and credits](/en/ia/creditos).

See [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the AI Assistant reply to my customers?">
    No. It only talks to you, inside the CRM. The one that replies to leads is the [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia), and only in WhatsApp conversations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it change something without me noticing?">
    No. Requests that change settings or the calendar go through the **Confirm change** window. The change only happens after you click **Apply**. When creating a product, the confirmation is the choice between **Upload photo** and **Create without photo**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find the AI Assistant in the menu. Why?">
    The item appears according to your access permission. Talk to the person in charge of your company's account. See [Team](/en/conta/equipe).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
