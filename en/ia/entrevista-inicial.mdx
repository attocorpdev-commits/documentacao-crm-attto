The onboarding interview is the assistant that creates your first customer service AI. You explain how your business works, by text, by voice or with documents, and the CRM turns the answers into the AI's personality and rules.

<Note>
  Creating your first AI does not take anything from your conversation balance. See [Conversations and AI credits](/en/ia/creditos).
</Note>

## Where the interview appears

- **On first access.** After you register the company, the creation of your AI opens. See [First access](/en/primeiros-passos/primeiro-acesso).
- **In AI Agent.** If your AI has not been set up yet, the screen shows **Let's create your AI**. Click **Create my AI**.

The first screen says **Let's get** followed by your company name and **ready in 2 minutes**. Click **Get started**. If you'd rather leave it for later, click **Set up later**.

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the pace">
    In **How do you want to set it up?**, choose **Quick onboarding** or **Advanced onboarding**. See the difference in [Quick or advanced](#quick-or-advanced).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to answer">
    In **How would you rather answer?**, choose **Answer by text**, **Answer by voice** or **Create from documents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your type of business">
    In **What's your type of business?**, pick an option. The CRM fills in part of the answers based on it, and you can adjust everything later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    The questions change depending on the pace and the way of answering you chose. See [The questions on each path](#the-questions-on-each-path).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the AI">
    On the **All set to create** screen, click **Generate my AI**. To check first, click **Review answers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the AI on">
    When **Your AI is ready!** appears, choose **Connect to my WhatsApp** or **Test in the Playground**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The available business types are **Clinic / Health**, **Real estate**, **E-commerce**, **Services**, **Courses / Info product**, **Funeral home**, **Agribusiness / Rural** and **Other**.

## Quick or advanced

| | **Quick onboarding** | **Advanced onboarding** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| For whom | Wants the AI live soon | Wants to fine-tune tone, limits and hours |
| Questions | Just the essentials | 42 questions, in three phases |
| The rest | Filled in based on your type of business | Comes from your answers |
| Documents | Not requested | Requested at the end, if you have them |

## The questions on each path

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Quick by text">
    One screen with three required questions:

    - **What do you sell or offer?**
    - **What should it try to achieve?**
    - **When to bring in a person**

    Tone of voice, limits and hours come pre-filled based on your type of business. Click **Review and create** to continue.

    If an answer is missing, the screen shows **Fill in to continue** and tells you which field is empty.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Quick by voice">
    You speak freely, in a single audio, answering a list of questions shown on screen.

    1. Read the tips and click **Got it, let's record**.
    2. Tap the microphone and talk like you're explaining to a friend. You have 5 minutes. If it doesn't fit, you can add 5 more at the end.
    3. Tap to finish. The CRM shows **Organizing what you said...**.
    4. If something essential was left out, **Just a couple of details left** appears. Record a short second audio answering only what's missing.
    5. On the **Here's what I understood** screen, check the summary and click **Continue**.

    If the CRM doesn't capture any audio, click **Re-record** or answer by text. Without microphone access, you can also continue by text.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Advanced">
    There are 42 questions, one per screen, split into three phases: **Essential**, **Important** and **Enrichment**. They cover the company, tone of voice, what you sell, your audience, the sales journey, the AI's rules, when to hand over to the team, operations and the most common objections.

    - Answer at least 30. The counter shows how many are left to reach the minimum.
    - Each answer can be up to 1,500 characters. Above that, the screen asks you to shorten it.
    - Click **Answer by audio** to speak instead of typing. Each audio can be up to 2 minutes, and the transcription shows up in the field for you to edit.
    - Use **Skip** to leave a question for later and **See all** to jump straight to any question.
    - Click **Finish** when you are done.

    In advanced mode, choosing **Answer by voice** leads to these same questions, with audio on each one.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From documents">
    Pick one or more files about your business, such as PDF, Word, spreadsheet or text. The CRM reads the documents, fills in the answers and takes you straight to the creation screen.

    If no file is read, **No document was read** appears. This happens with images, scanned PDFs or empty files. Try another file or answer by text.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Documents in advanced mode

After the questions, advanced mode shows **Documents (optional)**. You can send:

| Document | What the AI does with it |
| --- | --- |
| **Price list** | Reads it to answer and can send it to customers |
| **Product photos or brochure** | Can send it to customers |
| **Screenshots of ready messages** | Uses it as reference only. Never sends it to customers |

<Warning>
  If a file seems to contain private information, the CRM warns you. Remove that part of the file or click **I confirm it can be used**. Until this is resolved, the AI is not generated.
</Warning>

## What the interview generates

When you click **Generate my AI**, the CRM turns your answers into the text that guides the AI: how it talks, what it can and cannot do, and when it calls a person. The documents you sent go to the agent's **Documents** tab.

On the **Your AI is ready!** screen:

- **See what I taught the AI** shows the generated text.
- **Connect to my WhatsApp** takes you to connect the AI to a channel.
- **Test in the Playground** opens a test before you turn it on.

<Note>
  The AI only replies on the channels you connect. Your current customer service does not change until then.
</Note>

### About the cost

The first creation of each agent does not take anything from your balance. If a creation uses the balance, for example when generating again, the final screen tells you. If there is not enough balance, **No balance** appears. See [Conversations and AI credits](/en/ia/creditos).

## Stop and continue later

You can close the interview halfway. To come back, open [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) and click **Continue setup**. **Resuming your setup where you left off.** appears. If you want to start again, click **Start over**.

<Tip>
  In advanced mode, the answer draft is saved in the browser. If you switch computers or clear the browser, you may lose what has not been sent yet.
</Tip>

## Adjust the AI later

Everything the interview generates can be changed later, in [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia):

- **Personality tab:** click **Edit prompt** to change the text, or redo the questionnaire in the **Prompt Builder**.
- **Documents tab:** send, replace or remove files.
- **Reset the AI**, in the **General** tab: starts the setup over from scratch.

## Interview with Atton

Some accounts started building their AI in a conversation with Atton, by voice or text. In that case, two notices may appear:

- **Resume Atto's interview**: the interview was left halfway. Click **Resume** to continue where you left off.
- **Make your AI stronger**, on the Dashboard: the AI was created, but some items are missing. The notice lists what is missing. Click **Complete now**.

In this conversation, you choose **Answer by audio** or **Answer by text**. At the end, Atton shows what it understood in **Is this what I understood?** and asks for documents in **Your AI's documents**. Anything missing becomes pending, and you can send it later.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to answer everything at once?">
    No. You can close it and come back later with **Continue setup**. In advanced mode, you only need to answer 30 of the 42 questions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I picked the wrong type of business. What now?">
    Go back to the **What's your type of business?** screen with **Back** and pick again. Changing the type restarts the answers it filled in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A notice appeared about a questionnaire in progress at another company.">
    You started the interview in another company in your account. Click **Back to** the other company to continue there, or **Start over here**. Starting over deletes the answers in progress.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some essential details are missing appeared. What do I do?">
    Answers the AI needs to be useful are missing. Click **Review answers** and fill in what is empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the interview turn the AI on for my customers right away?">
    No. The AI only replies after you connect it to a channel, with **Connect to my WhatsApp** or in the agent's **Channels** tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
