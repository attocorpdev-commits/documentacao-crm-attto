The Atto Corp CRM has three plans: **Start**, **Prime** and **Ultra**.

You subscribe to Start and Prime on your own, right on the plans screen. **Ultra** is tailored: it is built for your operation and you sign up by talking to the Atto team.

This page shows what each plan includes, matching the comparison shown in the CRM. To manage your subscription, see [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).

<Note>
  This page does not list prices. To see them, go to [attocorp.com/#planos](https://attocorp.com/#planos).
</Note>

## Item-by-item comparison

The tables below follow the comparison on the plans screen, group by group. ✓ means included and ✗ means not included.

### Customer service

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| AI Agent replies 24/7 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Spots who is ready to buy | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Your team takes over the conversation in one click | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Explains your product and answers questions so you don't have to repeat yourself | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

- **AI Agent replies 24/7**: no queue and no business hours. If the monthly quota runs out and there is no extra pack, the AI pauses until the renewal or until you buy a pack.
- **Spots who is ready to buy**: the AI reads the conversation and classifies by the interest the person shows. It is not keywords or a fixed rule. When the sale closes outside the conversation (in person or by phone), the AI does not have that information.
- **Explains your product and answers questions**: it works from the knowledge base you feed it, such as product, price, policy and common objections. The better the material, the better the answer. See [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia).

### Volume

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Conversations per month | Plan quota | Plan quota | Unlimited, plan number or by scope |
| Extra conversation packs | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

- **Conversations per month**: on screen, each plan shows its number of conversations. 1 conversation is 1 contact within a 24-hour window. If the same contact comes back the next day, it counts again. On Ultra the volume is tailored: the screen shows **Unlimited** when there is no fixed quota.
- **Extra conversation packs**: you are notified when you get close to the monthly limit. The extra pack adds up and does not expire. See [AI conversations and credits](/en/ia/creditos).

### Funnel

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Contact management | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| AI moves the contact as it progresses | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |

- **Contact management**: a kanban-style funnel, with stages and triggers you set up.
- **AI moves the contact as it progresses**: the AI reads the conversation and moves the contact to another stage. It is not random or a fixed rule. When the sale closes outside the conversation, the AI does not have that information.

### Channels

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| WhatsApp (QR Code) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Official Meta API | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| WhatsApp numbers included | 1 | 1 | by scope |
| Instagram Direct | ✗ | add-on | 2 included |

- **Official Meta API**: this is the **WhatsApp Oficial (Meta)** type. There is no structural risk of the number being banned. The risk exists only if you break Meta's usage rules. See the difference in [WhatsApp types](/en/primeiros-passos/tipos-whatsapp).
- **WhatsApp numbers included**: Start and Prime come with 1 WhatsApp number each. On Ultra, the amount is set by scope. Need more numbers? Talk to us.
- **Instagram Direct**: a separate channel, with its own quota, that does not take up your WhatsApp numbers. It is an extra item on the Prime and Ultra plans. Ultra already includes 2 channels. Not available on Start. See [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram).

### Scheduling

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Scheduling calendar | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Sends payment link | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Notifies the team when the contact is ready | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

- **Scheduling calendar**: Start has the full Schedule, with booking page, manual booking and Google Calendar. What is exclusive to Prime is the AI booking on its own, from the conversation, without anyone typing.
- **Sends payment link**: the AI sends the link when it is set up and trained to do so.
- **Notifies the team when the contact is ready**: the alert arrives by WhatsApp and inside the CRM. On Start, you are notified of new leads and of people who showed interest (when the person asks about the product or service). On Prime, also of qualified leads, people with no reply and people who received a link.

### Data

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Real-time dashboard and metrics | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Report in plain business language | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |

- **Real-time dashboard and metrics**: contact volume, when contacts arrived, when messages were sent, Pipeline movement for the day and deal value.
- **Report in plain business language**: the main objections of the period and the arguments that worked best.

### Trust

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Team members | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited |
| No activation fee | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

To invite people and set what each one can do, see [Team](/en/conta/equipe).

### Tailored only

| Feature | Start | Prime | Ultra |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Weekly conversation audit | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Follow-up with people who stopped replying | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Integration with an API or external service | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Scope and volumes set after a diagnosis | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

- **Integration with an API or external service**: Ultra adapts to your operation. It can sync with your management system, another CRM or another service. The scope is defined together with the team.

## Monthly or annual

At the top of the plans screen you choose between **Monthly plan** and **Annual plan**.

- **Monthly plan**: you are charged every month, as a subscription. The card shows **no activation fee · cancel anytime**.
- **Annual plan**: a single upfront charge, by card or PIX, that covers 12 months. The amount equals 10 monthly fees, which is why the card shows **2 months free** and the discount percentage. The annual plan does not renew on its own.

<Note>
  The monthly amount shown on the **Annual plan** is the yearly total divided by 12. Prices are listed at [attocorp.com/#planos](https://attocorp.com/#planos).
</Note>

Already on the monthly plan? You can switch to annual. See [Switch from monthly to annual](/en/conta/planos#switch-from-monthly-to-annual).

To subscribe to any plan, check **I have read and accept the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.**, below the plans.

## 7-day free trial

The **7-day free trial** button shows on Start and Prime for people who never had a plan, and only on the **Monthly plan**.

- The trial is valid once per account.
- There is no trial on the **Annual plan**: the button is **Subscribe to this plan**.
- If you already subscribe, you see **Switch to this plan**. If you had a plan before, you see **Subscribe to this plan**.

Ultra has no free trial. Its button is **Talk to the team**, which opens a WhatsApp conversation with the Atto team.

## Extra items

- **Conversations**: if the monthly quota runs out, you buy an extra conversation pack. See how in [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) and [AI conversations and credits](/en/ia/creditos).
- **Instagram Direct**: an extra item on the Prime and Ultra plans. Ultra already includes 2 channels.
- **WhatsApp numbers**: Start and Prime include 1 number. For more numbers, talk to us through [Support](/en/empresa/suporte).
- **Members**: unlimited on every plan.
- **Companies**: if the screen shows **Plan for the company:**, each company has its own plan. Choose the company before subscribing.

## How to change plans

Plan changes are in **My Profile > Subscription > Change plan**. Upgrading takes effect right away. Downgrading is scheduled for the end of the cycle. The full step by step is in [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Start and Prime?">
    Both reply, qualify and hand the conversation over to your team. On Prime the AI also moves the contact between Pipeline stages, books on the calendar on its own from the conversation, and you get the **Report in plain business language**. Prime also notifies the team about more types of events and allows Instagram Direct as an extra item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What counts as a conversation?">
    1 contact served by the AI within a 24-hour window. If the same contact comes back the next day, it counts as a new conversation. See [AI conversations and credits](/en/ia/creditos).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused conversations carry over to the next month?">
    The monthly quota does not carry over. The extra conversation pack, on the other hand, adds up and does not expire.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the monthly conversations run out?">
    You are notified before you reach the limit. If the quota runs out and there is no extra pack, the AI pauses until the renewal or until you buy a pack.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Start plan include Instagram?">
    No. Instagram Direct is an extra item on the Prime and Ultra plans. See [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign up for Ultra?">
    On the plans screen, click **Talk to the team** on the Ultra card. A WhatsApp conversation opens with the Atto team, who builds the scope with you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
