This page shows how to sign in to the Atto Corp CRM for the first time. You also learn how to recover your password and find your way around the menu.

## How to sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login screen">
    Go to [app.attocorp.com](https://app.attocorp.com/auth). The screen shows the title **Welcome**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Type your **Email** and your **Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign in">
    You are taken straight into the system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Open the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.attocorp.com/auth" horizontal>
  Goes straight to the Atto Corp login screen.
</Card>

<Note>
  The **Sign in** button is only available after you answer the **Cookies & Privacy** notice, in the bottom left corner of the screen. Any choice unlocks the button: **Accept all**, **Reject non-essential** or **Save choices**, under **Preferences**. Necessary cookies (login, security and language) are always on. See [Policies](/en/empresa/politicas).
</Note>

<Warning>
  After several wrong attempts, the system shows **Too many attempts** and asks you to wait a few seconds before trying again.
</Warning>

### Security check

Right above the **Sign in** button there is a security check box. It confirms that the person signing in is a human and not a robot. The same box shows up on sign-up, on password recovery and on the password change in **My Profile**.

- Almost always it completes on its own in a few seconds. Meanwhile, the button shows **Verifying...**.
- Sometimes the box asks for a click. Click it and wait.
- If **We could not complete the security check. Please try again.** appears, click **Try again**, right below the message.
- If **We could not confirm the security check. Refresh the page and try again.** appears, reload the page and try again.

At the bottom of the sign-in screens there is the **Service status** link. It opens, in a new tab, the page that shows whether the CRM is working normally.

### If someone from your company invited you

People added to the team receive a welcome email with a temporary password.

On first access, the **Set your password** screen appears. Fill in **New password** and **Confirm password** and click **Save password**. The password must have at least 10 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character. The **Password requirements** list shows what is still missing.

## I forgot my password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the code">
    On the login screen, click **I forgot my password**. Type your **Email** and click **Send code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">
    You receive a 6-digit code by email. Type the code and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the new password">
    Fill in **New password** and **Confirm new password**. The password must have at least 10 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character. It cannot be a very common password or contain your name or email. The list below the field turns green as each rule is met. Click **Reset password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with the new password">
    Go back to the login screen and sign in as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Didn't get the code? Wait for the countdown and click **Resend code**.
</Tip>

## Initial setup

When the account is new, the CRM guides you through three moments, in this order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell us about your company">
    The CRM opens the **Welcome! Set up your company** window. Enter the **Company name** and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your AI">
    Next, the setup for your customer service AI opens. Click **Get started** to set it up now or **Set up later**. See the details in [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the tour">
    Finally, the CRM shows a quick tour of the main screens. To see it again later, open the profile menu and click **Redo tour**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  To serve customers, you still need to connect a number. See [Connect your WhatsApp](/en/primeiros-passos/conectar-whatsapp).
</Note>

## Get to know the menu

The menu is on the left side. It is divided into groups. Click a group name to open or close its list.

| Group | Items |
| --- | --- |
| **Main** | **Dashboard**, **AI Assistant** |
| **Support** | **WhatsApp**, **Instagram** |
| **CRM** | **Pipeline**, **Activities**, **Contacts**, **Tags**, **Quick replies**, **Calendar**, **Weekly Report**, **Manager Area** |
| **Marketing** | **Broadcasts**, **Collections** |
| **Settings** | **Connections**, **Company Team** |

At the top of the menu you find:

- The company selector, with the text **Select company**. Use it to switch companies or create a **New company**.
- The **AI Agent** card, which takes you to your customer service AI setup.
- The **Collapse** button, which shrinks the menu. To open it again, click **Expand**.

In the top right corner of the screen are the **Support** button, the notification bell and your photo or initial. Click the photo to open **My Profile**, **Settings**, **Redo tour**, **Service status**, **Check for update** and **Sign out**. **Service status** opens, in a new tab, the page that shows whether the CRM is working normally. In the same menu you choose your **Status** (**Online**, **Away** or **Offline**) and the screen **Theme**.

## Where to get help

The **Support** button opens a window with three paths:

- **Updates**, with the latest news about the system.
- **Documentation**, which opens this help center.
- A **DESCRIBE THE PROBLEM** field, to talk to a person on the support team. Write the problem and enter the email you use in the system.

<Tip>
  Clicking the Atto Corp logo at the top of the menu takes you to the [attocorp.com](https://attocorp.com) website, not to a screen in the system. To go back to the home screen, click **Dashboard**.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The menu items depend on your plan and your access permission. If an item doesn't show up for you, talk to the person in charge of your company's account.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I see the system's guided walkthrough again?">
    Click your photo or initial in the top right corner and choose **Redo tour**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got an invalid or expired link message. What do I do?">
    Click **Go to login** and request the recovery again in **I forgot my password**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The system says the code is incorrect.">
    The code may have been typed wrong or may have expired. Check the 6 digits or click **Resend code**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign out of my account?">
    Click your photo or initial and choose **Sign out**. Confirm with **Sign out** in the window that appears.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
