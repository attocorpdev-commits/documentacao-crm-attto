This page gathers the most common CRM notices and problems. Each row shows what appears on the screen, what to do and where to read more.

<Tip>
  Search for the exact message you saw. Use **Ctrl + F** (or **Cmd + F** on a Mac) to find it on this page, or use the help center search at the top of the screen.
</Tip>

## Access and sign-in

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Could not sign in** with "Check your details and try again." | Check the email and password you typed. If you don't remember the password, click **I forgot my password** on the sign-in screen. | [Recover your password](/en/conta/recuperar-senha) |
| The **Sign in** button is not available | Accept the session cookies in the notice in the bottom left corner of the screen. The button unlocks after that. | [First access](/en/primeiros-passos/primeiro-acesso) |
| **Too many attempts** | The system pauses sign-in after several wrong attempts. Wait a few seconds and try again. | [First access](/en/primeiros-passos/primeiro-acesso) |
| **Invalid or expired link** message | Click **Go to login** and request the recovery again in **I forgot my password**. | [Recover your password](/en/conta/recuperar-senha) |
| **Wrong code** when recovering your password | Check the 6 digits sent by email. If the code expired, click **Resend code**. | [Recover your password](/en/conta/recuperar-senha) |
| The **Set your password** screen appeared | Fill in **New password** and **Confirm password**, with at least 8 characters, and click **Save password**. The CRM only opens after that. | [Profile](/en/conta/perfil) |
| **Invalid or expired code. Check the app and try again.** | Use the code that shows in the authenticator app right now. Set your phone's date and time to automatic. | [Two-step verification](/en/conta/seguranca) |
| I changed or lost the phone with the authenticator app | Without the app, the CRM does not allow access. Contact support and include your account email. | [Two-step verification](/en/conta/seguranca) |

## WhatsApp (QR Code)

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An error instead of the QR Code | Click **Try again**. The CRM generates a new QR Code. | [Connect your WhatsApp](/en/primeiros-passos/conectar-whatsapp) |
| **One number needs to be reconnected** or the **Needs reconnection** badge | Click **Reconnect**. If it does not come back, scan the QR Code again. | [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) |
| **Number disconnected** window (or **Numbers disconnected**) | The CRM tried to reconnect on its own and could not. Click **Go to Connections** and reconnect the number. | [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) |
| **WhatsApp session may be out of sync** window | Some messages may not reach the customer. Click **Sync** and reconnect the number. **Dismiss** only hides the notice. | [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) |
| A message saying the **channel limit was reached** | Each plan has a channel limit. To connect more numbers, click **Talk to support** in that same window. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |
| I don't see the **Add channel** button | Your user does not have permission to create channels. Ask the account owner to grant it on the **Company Team** screen. | [Team](/en/conta/equipe) |
| The message had two ticks and now shows an alert | WhatsApp accepted the message and then rejected it. Click the alert icon to try again. | [WhatsApp](/en/atendimento/whatsapp) |
| The conversation disappeared from the list | Check the filter, the chosen channel and whether **Showing unread only** is on. Go back to **All** and **All channels**. | [WhatsApp](/en/atendimento/whatsapp) |

## Official WhatsApp (Meta)

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Outside the 24h window**: the customer must reply to reopen the conversation | After 24 hours from the customer's last message, you can only send an approved template. Send a template or wait for the customer to reply. | [24-hour window](/en/atendimento/janela-24h) |
| **Resend using the template** when trying to resend | The failed message was a template. Choose the template again in the **Send Template** window. | [WhatsApp templates](/en/atendimento/templates-whatsapp) |
| Template with the **Rejected** status | Meta did not accept the template. Review the text and create it again. | [WhatsApp templates](/en/atendimento/templates-whatsapp) |
| I can't see a channel's templates | The channel may be disconnected. In the channel's three dots, choose **Reconnect** and then click **Refresh**. | [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) |
| I connected the wrong Meta account | In the channel's three dots, choose **Reauthorize (wrong account)** and confirm with **Remove and reconnect**. The conversations are kept. | [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) |
| **Plan channel limit reached** window | The window shows how many channels your plan allows. Click **Buy extra channel** to connect more. | [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) |
| **WhatsApp delivery issue**, with a pending payment notice from Meta | Settle the account payment with Meta. Once messages are delivered again, click **Resolved** to remove the notice. | [Support](/en/empresa/suporte) |

## Instagram

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red message: **Another app connected to Instagram controls this conversation.** | Another tool linked to your Instagram took over the conversation. The fix is a one-time setting in Meta. | [Instagram: messages refused by another app](/en/atendimento/instagram-mensagens-recusadas) |
| **Outside Instagram's 24 h window** | Meta only allows a direct message up to 24 hours after the person's last message. Ask the person to send a message on Direct and reply within that time. | [24-hour window](/en/atendimento/janela-24h) |
| The screen says I have no Instagram channel assigned | Your user does not have access to an Instagram account yet. Ask the person in charge of your company's account to give you access. | [Team](/en/conta/equipe) |
| Image, video or audio unavailable | The CRM could not load this content. Open the conversation directly on Instagram to see it. | [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram) |
| The AI does not reply on Instagram | This is expected. The integrated AI only handles WhatsApp conversations. On Instagram, your team handles the service. | [Instagram](/en/atendimento/instagram) |
| I can't get an Instagram channel on the Start plan | The Instagram channel belongs to the **Prime** and **Ultra** plans. Change your plan to use it. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |

## AI Agent

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The AI stopped replying | Check the switch on the **General** tab, the **Turn the AI off for all conversations** switch, the channel switch on the **Channels** tab, the business hours and the **Credit balance**. | [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) |
| **Your conversations ran out** | The balance ran out and the AI stops replying until you top up. Your team keeps working as normal. Top up for the AI to come back. | [Conversations and AI credits](/en/ia/creditos) |
| **Your AI has no connected channel** | No number is served by the AI. Click **Activate number** to turn on a channel, or **Check connections**. | [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) |
| **AI globally off** badge at the top of the conversation | Someone turned the AI off for all conversations. To allow it only there, use **Turn the AI on only in this conversation (AI globally off)**. | [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) |
| I can't turn on the AI on a channel | The agent must be active and have an active personality. If the channel already has another AI turned on, turn the other one off first. | [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) |
| Someone took over the conversation and the AI won't come back | Open the conversation and click **Resume integrated AI in this conversation**. The CRM confirms with **The conversation is back with the AI.** | [AI Agent](/en/ia/agente-ia) |
| The AI replies, but doesn't apply tags, schedule or send files | These actions belong to the Prime and Ultra plans. On Start, the AI only chats, qualifies and hands off to a person. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |
| I don't see the AI buttons in the conversation | The controls depend on your plan and your permission. Talk to the person in charge of your company's account. | [Team](/en/conta/equipe) |

## Broadcasts

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign stuck on **Waiting for window** | The current time is outside the allowed send window. The broadcast resumes on its own at the time shown. | [Broadcasts](/en/marketing/disparos) |
| **Template variable not filled in** in the report | The CRM blocked the send before it went out, so nobody received the raw variable. Fix the message and send again to those contacts. | [Broadcasts](/en/marketing/disparos) |
| A contact with the **Unsubscribed** label can't be selected | The person asked to leave the list or the number does not have WhatsApp. They stay out of broadcasts, even with **Select all**. | [Broadcasts](/en/marketing/disparos) |
| A channel showing **(disconnected)** in **Sending channel** | The chosen channel is off. Reconnect the number before starting the campaign. | [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes) |

## Plans and payment

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirming your payment** | This is normal. Activation takes a few seconds and the screen updates on its own. If it does not activate within a few minutes, contact support. Do not pay again. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |
| **Choose your plan** screen | The account has no active plan. Choose a plan to unlock access. Your data stays saved. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |
| **Settle your subscription** screen | There is a pending charge. Subscribe to a plan to reactivate access. Your data stays saved. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |
| I paid for the top-up and the balance did not change | Confirmation can take a few moments. If it does not update within a few minutes, contact support with the email used for the purchase. Do not pay again. | [Conversations and AI credits](/en/ia/creditos) |
| The free trial button is gone and **Subscribe to this plan** shows instead | The free trial is valid once per account. To continue, choose a plan. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |
| I downgraded my plan and nothing changed | The downgrade is scheduled for the end of the current cycle. Until then you use your current plan. | [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos) |

## Team and permissions

| What you see | What to do | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Member limit reached** | Each plan allows a certain number of members. To increase it, click **Contact support**. | [Team](/en/conta/equipe) |
| The system says the person is already a member | That email is already part of the team. Find the person in the list and edit their permissions. | [Team](/en/conta/equipe) |
| A member with the **No channel assigned** notice | Edit the member and, for each channel, choose **All** or **Mine** so they can see the conversations. | [Team](/en/conta/equipe) |
| I can't see all of the company's contacts | Depending on your permission, you only see the contacts from the channels assigned to you. | [Contacts](/en/crm/contatos) |
| The **Import CSV** tab is disabled | Importing requires full access to Contacts. Ask the account owner to give you access. | [Import contacts](/en/crm/importar-contatos) |
| **Are you still there?** window | Click **Continue**. With no answer within 2 minutes, your status changes to **Away** and you stop receiving new conversations. | [Away status](/en/atendimento/status-ausente) |
| I stopped receiving new conversations | Check your presence status. While **Away**, you do not receive new conversations. Also check your place in the rotation. | [Queue and rotation](/en/atendimento/fila-rodizio) |
