To serve customers through the CRM, you need to connect at least one number. There are two types of connection. If you are not sure which one to use yet, see [WhatsApp types](/en/primeiros-passos/tipos-whatsapp).

## How to access

In the side menu, open **Settings** and click **Connections**. In the **WhatsApp** group, choose the card for the type you want to connect: **WhatsApp** (by QR Code) or **Official WhatsApp (Meta)**.

<Card title="Open Connections in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/connections">
  Goes straight to the screen. You need to be signed in to your CRM account.
</Card>

## Step by step

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WhatsApp (QR Code)" icon="qrcode">
    Have the phone with the number's WhatsApp at hand and use the CRM on your computer, so you can scan the screen.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add a channel">
        On the **WhatsApp** card, click **Add channel**. If there is no number yet, you can also click **Connect number**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name the channel">
        In **Channel name**, write something that helps identify the number. Examples: Sales, Support, Finance. Click **Create channel**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Generate the QR Code">
        The channel shows up in the list with the **Connect WhatsApp** area open. Click **Connect WhatsApp** to generate the QR Code.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Scan it with your phone">
        On your phone, open WhatsApp, go to **Linked devices** and scan the QR Code on the screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check the connection">
        The CRM shows the message **Channel connected successfully!**. The channel now displays the **Connected** badge and the phone number.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      The QR Code expires in 60 seconds. The screen shows the countdown in **QR expires in**. To swap the QR before it runs out, click **Generate new QR**. If time runs out, click **Try again**.
    </Warning>

    <Tip>
      If the QR Code area is closed, click the QR Code icon next to the channel name to open it again.
    </Tip>

    <Card title="Open WhatsApp (QR Code) in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/connections/whatsapp">
      Goes straight to the list of QR Code channels.
    </Card>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Official WhatsApp (Meta)" icon="badge-check">
    The number must be active at Meta, with the fee paid. Without that, use **Add** and paste the channel token.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start the connection">
        On the **Official WhatsApp (Meta)** card, in the **Channels** tab, click **Connect via Meta**. In the window that opens, click **Connect via Meta** again.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        In the **Confirm channel activation** window, click **Yes, connect**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize in the Meta window">
        A Meta window opens. Choose the account and authorize. You don't need to paste a token.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish">
        When **Channel connected successfully!** appears, click **Done**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      If your browser blocks the window, allow pop-ups for the site and click **Try again**. If you prefer help, click **Do it with support**.
    </Tip>

    After connecting, create the templates in the **Templates** tab. They are needed to talk to contacts outside the 24-hour window. See [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes).

    <Card title="Open Official WhatsApp (Meta) in the CRM" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" horizontal href="https://app.attocorp.com/app/connections/whatsapp-oficial/canais">
      Goes straight to the list of official channels.
    </Card>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## After connecting

Messages that arrive on both types of number show up in **WhatsApp**, in the **Support** group of the menu.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I got a message saying the channel limit was reached.">
    Each plan has a channel limit for each type of WhatsApp. To connect more numbers, click **Talk to support** in that same window. See [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the Add channel button.">
    Your user does not have permission to create channels. Ask the account owner to grant this permission on the **Company Team** screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp (QR Code): an error appeared instead of the QR Code.">
    Click **Try again**. The CRM generates a new QR Code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp (QR Code): my number shows the Needs reconnection notice.">
    Click **Reconnect**. If that doesn't solve it, open the channel's QR Code and scan it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp (QR Code): the WhatsApp session may be out of sync window appeared.">
    The number stays connected, but some of the messages you send may not reach the customer. Click **Sync** to go to the channel list and reconnect the number, or **Dismiss** to close the notice. In the channel list, this number shows the **Session may be out of sync** badge. Learn more in [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Official WhatsApp (Meta): I connected the wrong account.">
    In the channel's three dots menu, use **Reauthorize (wrong account)** and choose the right account at Meta. See [Connections](/en/conta/conexoes).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
