The CRM connects WhatsApp numbers in two ways. Both work from the same conversations screen, but they behave differently.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="WhatsApp (QR Code)" icon="qrcode">
    You connect the number by scanning a QR Code with your phone, just like WhatsApp Web.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Official WhatsApp (Meta)" icon="badge-check">
    The number is connected through Meta's official API, with authorization done on Meta itself.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Comparison

| | WhatsApp (QR Code) | Official WhatsApp (Meta) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How it connects** | By scanning the QR Code with your phone | By authorizing through Meta, or by pasting the channel token |
| **24-hour window** | None. You reply whenever you want | After 24 hours without a message from the contact, only approved templates can be sent |
| **Templates** | Not used | Uses templates approved by Meta |
| **Broadcasts** | Free text, with an interval in minutes | Approved template, sent in batches |
| **Message reactions** | Yes | No |
| **AI Agent** | Yes | Yes |
| **Risk of being banned** | Subject to bans at high volume | Stable and with no risk of being banned |
| **Meta costs** | Does not apply | Meta charges for usage and can limit the channel or make it more expensive at any time |
| **When the number drops** | Reconnect and, if needed, scan the QR Code again | Reconnect from the screen, without using a new plan slot |
| **Plan limit** | Its own channel limit | Its own channel limit |

## Which one to choose

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp (QR Code)" icon="qrcode">
    Good for getting started quickly with the number you already use on your phone. It takes a few minutes and does not depend on Meta approval.

    Because it is not an official channel, the connection can be blocked or banned by WhatsApp, especially with high-volume sending, and there is no availability guarantee.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Official WhatsApp (Meta)" icon="badge-check">
    Recommended for higher volume and for those who need to send messages outside the 24-hour window, with approved templates.

    To connect, the number must be active on Meta, with the fee paid.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Atto Corp recommends Official WhatsApp (Meta) for operations with many contacts or frequent campaigns.
</Tip>

You can have both types at the same time, within each one's channel limit on your plan. See [Plans and billing](/en/conta/planos).
